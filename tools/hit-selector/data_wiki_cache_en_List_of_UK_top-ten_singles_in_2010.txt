[[File:Rihanna a bercy.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Rihanna]] had the biggest selling single of 2010, "[[Love the Way You Lie]]" with [[Eminem]], which peaked at number two. It was one of six top 10 hits for Rihanna during the year, including number-ones "[[Only Girl (In the World)]]" and "[[What's My Name? (Rihanna song)|What's My Name?]]" (which topped the chart in January 2011).]]
[[File:Matt Cardle, Glasgow 2013.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Matt Cardle]], the winner of ''[[The X Factor (UK series 7)|the seventh series of The X Factor]]'', covered [[Biffy Clyro]]'s "[[Many of Horror]]" under the new title "When We Collide" and it became both the second best-selling song of the year and the [[List of Christmas number-one singles (UK)|Christmas number-one]].]]
[[File:Bruno Mars, Las Vegas 2010.jpg|200px|right|thumb|alt=|[[Bruno Mars]] made the UK top 10 for the first time in 2010 with three singles making the countdown, including the number-one hits "[[Nothin' on You]]" (with [[B.o.B]]) and "[[Just the Way You Are (Bruno Mars song)|Just the Way You Are]]" (the third best seller of the year).]]
The [[UK Singles Chart]] is one of many music charts compiled by the [[Official Charts Company]] that calculates the best-selling singles of the week in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.theofficialcharts.com/ |title=The Official UK Charts Company |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |access-date=1 January 2012}}</ref> Since 2004 the chart has been based on the sales of both [[CD single|physical singles]] and [[music download|digital downloads]], with [[airplay]] figures excluded from the official chart.<ref>{{cite book|title=[[Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums|Guinness World Records: British Hit Singles and Albums]]|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2005|isbn=1-904994-00-8|pages=14|publisher=[[HiT Entertainment|Guinness World Records Limited]]|edition=18th}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3196892.stm |title=New singles formats to save the charts
|work=[[BBC News]]|date=16 October 2003 |access-date=21 February 2010}}</ref> This list shows singles that peaked in the Top 10 of the [[UK Singles Chart]] during [[2010 in British music charts|2010]], as well as singles which peaked in [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2009|2009]] and [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2011|2011]] but were in the top 10 in 2010. The entry date is when the song appeared in the top 10 for the first time (week ending, as published by the Official Charts Company, which is six days after the chart is announced).

One-hundred and fifty-two singles were in the top ten in 2010. Nine singles from 2009 remained in the top 10 for several weeks at the beginning of the year, while "[[What's My Name? (Rihanna song)|What's My Name?]]" by [[Rihanna]] featuring [[Drake (rapper)|Drake]]" and "[[Who's That Chick?]]" by [[David Guetta]] featuring Rihanna were both released in 2010 but did not reach their peak until 2011.. "[[Tik Tok (song)|TiK ToK]]" by [[Kesha]], "[[Starstrukk]]" by [[3OH!3]] featuring [[Katy Perry]], "[[Don't Stop Believing]]" by [[Journey (band)|Journey]], "[[Look for Me]]" by [[Chipmunk (rapper)|Chipmunk]] featuring [[Talay Riley]], "[[The Climb (Miley Cyrus song)|The Climb]]" by [[Joe McElderry]] and "[[3 Words (song)|3 Words]]" by [[Cheryl Cole]] featuring [[will.i.am]] were the singles from 2009 to reach their peak in 2010. Forty-six artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2010. [[Ellie Goulding]], [[Justin Bieber]], [[Nicki Minaj]], [[Tinie Tempah]] and [[The Wanted]] were among the many artists who achieved their first UK charting top 10 single in 2010.

The first number-one single of the year was "The Climb" by ''[[The X Factor (UK)|X Factor]]'' [[The X Factor (UK series 6)|series 6]] winner [[Joe McElderry]]. Overall, thirty-five different singles peaked at number-one in 2010, with JLS (3, including the charity single "[[Everybody Hurts#Helping Haiti version|Everybody Hurts]]" with Helping Haiti) having the most singles hit that position.

==Background==
===Multiple entries===
One-hundred and fifty-two singles charted in the top 10 in 2010, with one-hundred and forty-five singles reaching their peak this year.

Forty-six artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2010. [[Rihanna]] secured the record for most top 10 hits in 2010 with six hit singles.

===Chart debuts===
Seventy-one artists achieved their first top 10 single in 2010, either as a lead or featured artist. Of these, fifteen went on to record another hit single that year: [[B.o.B]], [[Drake (musician)|Drake]], [[Ellie Goulding]], [[Emma's Imagination]], [[Example (musician)|Example]], [[Florence and the Machine]], [[Iyaz]], [[Justin Bieber]], [[Labrinth]], [[Matt Cardle]], [[Nicki Minaj]], [[Professor Green]], [[Roll Deep]], [[Swedish House Mafia]] and [[The Wanted]]. [[Bruno Mars]] and [[Glee Cast]] scored two more top 10 singles in 2010. [[Tinie Tempah]] had three other entries in his breakthrough year.

;Notes
Although [[Jedward]] scored their first top 10 hit in 2010 with "[[Under Pressure (Ice Ice Baby)]]" they were part of [[The X Factor (UK)|The X Factor]] [[The X Factor (UK series 6)|series 6]] finalists, who reached number-one with "[[You Are Not Alone]]". [[Olly Murs]], who also featured on this single, had his first two top 10 entries in his own right, namely debut "[[Please Don't Let Me Go]]" and "[[Thinking of Me]]". [[Diana Vickers]] peaked at number one with "[[Once (Diana Vickers song)|Once]]" in 2010 but had appeared on The X Factor in 2008 and, along with her fellow contestants, landed the top spot that same year with their cover of [[Mariah Carey]]'s "[[Hero (Mariah Carey song)|Hero]]".

[[McLean (singer)|McLean]] had his first solo hit single, "[[Your Name]]", this year but he had a previous chart credit on "[[I Got Soul]]" with [[Young Soul Rebels]]. [[Matt Cardle]] sang on "[[Heroes (David Bowie song)#The X Factor 2010 Finalists version|Heroes]]" with his fellow ''[[The X Factor (UK)|X Factor]]'' [[The X Factor (UK series 7)|series seven]] contestants prior to his debut solo hit "[[When We Collide]]". [[Cher Lloyd]], [[One Direction]] and [[Olly Murs]] would both go on to record top 10 singles in later years.

[[Travie McCoy]] of [[Gym Class Heroes]] had his first independent top 10 hit, collaborating with [[B.o.B.]] on "[[Billionaire (song)|Billionaire]]". [[Brandon Flowers]] had multiple top 10 singles as part of [[The Killers]] and achieved the first under his own name in 2010 with "[[Crossfire (Brandon Flowers song)|Crossfire]]", reaching a number 8 peak.

===Songs from films===
Original songs from various films entered the top 10 throughout the year. These included "[[We Dance On]]" (from ''[[StreetDance 3D]]'') and "[[Club Can't Handle Me]]" (''[[Step Up 3D]]'').

===Charity singles===
A number of singles recorded for charity reached the top ten in the charts in 2010. These included "[[Everybody Hurts]]" by [[Helping Haiti]] and "[[Heroes (David Bowie song)#The X Factor 2010 finalists version|Heroes]]"
by ''{{sortname|The|X Factor Finalists 2010|List of The X Factor finalists (UK series 7)}}''.

===Best-selling singles===
[[Eminem]] featuring [[Rihanna]] had the best-selling single of the year with "[[Love the Way You Lie]]". The song spent fourteen weeks in the top 10 (peaking at number two), sold over 854,000 copies and was certified platinum by the [[British Phonographic Institute|BPI]]. "[[Many of Horror#When We Collide|When We Collide]]" by [[Matt Cardle]] came in second place, selling more than 814,000 copies and losing out by around 40,000 sales. [[Bruno Mars]]'s "[[Just the Way You Are (Bruno Mars song)|Just the Way You Are]], "[[Only Girl in the World]]" from Rihanna and "[[OMG (Usher song)|OMG]]" by [[Usher (singer)|Usher]] featuring [[will.i.am]] made up the top five. Singles by [[Owl City]], [[B.o.B]] featuring [[Hayley Williams]], [[Katy Perry]] featuring [[Snoop Dogg]], [[Yolanda Be Cool]] & [[DCUP]] and [[Tinie Tempah]] were also in the [[2010 in British music charts#Best-selling singles of 2010|top ten best-selling singles]] of the year.

==Entries by artist==
{{see also|List of UK Singles Chart number ones of the 2010s#2010}}
The following table shows artists who achieved two or more top 10 entries in 2010, including singles that reached their peak in 2009 or 2011. The figures include both main artists and featured artists, while appearances on ensemble charity records are also counted for each artist. The total number of weeks an artist spent in the top ten in 2010 is also shown.
[[File:Katy Perry's show in Berlin (edit).JPG|150px|right|thumb|alt=|[[Katy Perry]] had five UK top 10 singles this year, the most successful of which was the number-one hit "[[California Gurls]]", which features guest vocals from [[Snoop Dogg]].]]
[[File:Tinie Tempah.jpg|150px|right|thumb|alt=|[[Tinie Tempah]] made his UK top 10 debut in 2010 with four singles making the countdown. Two of these reached number-one: "[[Pass Out (Tinie Tempah song)|Pass Out]]" and "[[Written in the Stars (Tinie Tempah song)|Written in the Stars]]" (which features vocals from American singer [[Eric Turner (singer)|Eric Turner]]).]]
[[File:Jason-Derulo by-Adam-Bielawski 2010-01-12.jpg|150px|thumb|right|alt=|American singer [[Jason Derulo]] scored two top 10 entries this year, including "[[In My Head (Jason Derulo song)|In My Head]]", which became his first UK number-one single.]]
[[File:Olly Murs-9858 (cropped).jpg|150px|thumb|right|alt=|[[Olly Murs]], who finished runner-up in ''[[The X Factor (UK series 6)|the sixth series of The X Factor]]'', made the UK top 10 for the first time this year, scoring two entries, including his debut single "[[Please Don't Let Me Go]]", which reached number-one in September.]]

* {{note label|ReEntry|A|a}} "One Shot" re-entered the top 10 at number 10 on 6 March 2010 (week ending) following the physical release.
* {{note label|CharitySingles|B|b}} Released as a "Charity Single" towards the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]].
* {{note label|BRITSingle|C|c}} Released as a single towards [[British Phonographic Industry#The BRIT Trust|The BRIT Trust]].
* {{note label|ReEntry|D|d}} "Gave It All Away" re-entered the top 10 at number 10 on 3 April 2010 (week ending).
* {{note label|ReEntry|E|e}} "OMG" re-entered the top 10 at number 6 on 19 June 2010 (week ending) following a performance on [[Britain's Got Talent (series 4)|Britain's Got Talent]].
* {{note label|ReEntry|F|f}} "The Best" originally peaked at number 5 in 1989, but re-entered the top 10 following a campaign by [[Rangers F.C.]] fans.
* {{note label|ReEntry|G|g|}} "Three Lions" originally peaked at number 1 in 1996, but re-entered the top 10 due to the [[World Cup 2010|World Cup]].
* {{note label|ReEntry|H|h}} "DJ Got Us Falling in Love" re-entered the top 10 at number 10 on 23 October 2010 (week ending) following a performance on [[The X Factor (UK series 7)|The X Factor]].
* {{note label|ReEntry|I|i}} "Make You Feel My Love" re-entered the top 10 at number 9 on 19 November 2010 (week ending) following a performance from [[Rebecca Ferguson (singer)|Rebecca Ferguson]] on The X Factor. The single re-entered for a second time on 4 December 2010 following the use of the song on The X Factor. It re-entered the top 10 for a third time at number 7 on 16 January 2011 following usage on [[Dancing on Ice]].
* {{note label|ReEntry|J|j}} "The Time (Dirty Bit)" re-entered the top 10 at number 6 on 11 December 2010 (week ending) following the release of the album ''[[The Beginning (The Black Eyed Peas album)|The Beginning]]''.
* {{note label|ReEntry|K|k}} "The Flood" re-entered the top 10 at number 6 on 25 December 2010 (week ending) following a performance on The X Factor Final.
* {{note label|ReEntry|L|l}} "Firework" re-entered the Top 10 at number 8 and "Like a G6" re-entered at number 10; both on 1 January 2011 (week ending).

*{{note label|FT|S|s}} Figure includes appearances on Taio Cruz's "Dirty Picture" and 3OH!3's "My First Kiss".
