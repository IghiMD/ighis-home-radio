''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine each year releases a Top [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] songs of the year, counted from the first week of November to the final week in October. For 2009, the chart was published on December 11.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2009/hot-100-songs/|title=Hot 100 Songs - Year-End 2008|year=2009|magazine=Billboard|access-date=July 23, 2023}}</ref> The [[2009 in music|2009]] list was dominated by [[The Black Eyed Peas]] and [[Lady Gaga]], who shared the top four spots. In late December, [[DJ Earworm]] released a [[Mashup (music)|mashup]] video to YouTube titled "Blame It On the Pop", featuring the top twenty-five songs from the list, as he had also done the previous two years for his [[DJ Earworm#"United State of Pop" series|"United State of Pop" series]]. The video quickly received four million views in little over a week.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.macon.com/248/story/976100.html|title=Viral Video: 2009's Top 25 songs remixed|last=Smith|first=Mark W.|date=6 January 2010|work=[[The Telegraph (Macon)]]|access-date=10 January 2010}} {{Dead link|date=October 2010|bot=H3llBot}}</ref>
[[File:Black Eyed Peas performing - 2006 - JD.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[The Black Eyed Peas]] had the best-performing single of the year with "[[Boom Boom Pow]]." They also had "[[I Gotta Feeling]]" on the chart at number 4.]]
[[File:Lady Gaga performing Eh, Eh (Nothing Else I Can Say) on The Monster Ball Tour, Ottawa, November 2009.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Pop singer [[Lady Gaga]] had four songs on the chart. "[[Paparazzi (Lady Gaga song)|Paparazzi]],"  "[[LoveGame]],"  "[[Just Dance (song)|Just Dance]]," and "[[Poker Face (song)|Poker Face]]" at 53, 35, 3, and 2 respectively.]]
[[File:Taylor Swift 2009 MTV VMA.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Taylor Swift]] was the most successful country artist, with three singles on the chart: [[Love Story (Taylor Swift song)|"Love Story"]] at 5, "[[You Belong With Me]]" at 11, and [[White Horse (Taylor Swift song)|"White Horse"]] at 76.]]
[[File:Beyonce-2008 2.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Beyoncé Knowles|Beyoncé]] had five songs on the chart, all of which were from her album ''[[I Am... Sasha Fierce]].'']]
[[File:Kanye West 2007.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Kanye West]] was the most successful male artist with five songs on the chart. His most successful single was "[[Heartless (Kanye West song)|Heartless]]" at number 9.]]
[[File:Rihanna AMA 2009 Red carpet.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Rihanna (singer)|Rihanna]] had three singles on the chart: [[Disturbia (song)|Disturbia]] at 77, [[Run This Town]] at 31, and  [[Live Your Life (T.I. song)|Live Your Life]] at 18.]]
[[File:Lil Wayne cropped.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Lil Wayne]] was another successful male artist with five songs on the chart. His most successful singles include "[[Down (Jay Sean song)|Down]]" with [[Jay Sean]] at number 20, "[[Let It Rock (Kevin Rudolf song)|Let It Rock]]" with [[Kevin Rudolf]] at 32, "[[Turnin Me On]]" with [[Keri Hilson]] at 49, "[[Every Girl (Young Money song)|Every Girl]]" with [[Young Money]] at 67, and "[[Forever (Drake, Kanye West, Lil Wayne, and Eminem song)|Forever]]" with [[Drake (musician)|Drake]], [[Kanye West]], and [[Eminem]] at 88.]]
[[File:T.I. performing in concert, wearing a Phoenix Suns cap (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[T.I.]] had three songs on the chart. His most successful singles include "[[Dead and Gone]]" with [[Justin Timberlake]] at number 12, "[[Live Your Life (T.I. song)|Live Your Life]]" with [[Rihanna]] at 18, and "[[Whatever You Like]]" at 40.]]
[[File:Britney Spears.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Britney Spears]] had four songs on the chart. Three singles from her album ''[[Circus (Britney Spears album)|Circus]]'': "[[Circus (Britney Spears song)|Circus]]" at 27, "[[Womanizer (song)|Womanizer]]" at 39, "[[If U Seek Amy]]" at 74, and "[[3 (Britney Spears song)|3]]" at 87 from her album ''[[The Singles Collection (Britney Spears album)|The Singles Collection]]''.]]
