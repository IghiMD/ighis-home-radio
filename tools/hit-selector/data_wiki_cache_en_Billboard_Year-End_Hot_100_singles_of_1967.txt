[[File:Fanclub1965Lulu-cropped.jpg|thumb|right|190px|"[[To Sir with Love (song)|To Sir With Love]]" by [[Lulu (singer)|Lulu]] (''pictured'') was the number one song of 1967.]]

[[File:The Monkees.jpg|thumb|[[The Monkees]] (''pictured'') had four songs on the year-end chart ("[[I'm a Believer]]" at number five, "[[A Little Bit Me, a Little Bit You]]" at number 60, "[[Pleasant Valley Sunday]]" at number 74, and "[[Daydream Believer]]" at number 94), the most of any artist that year.]]

[[File:The Supremes 1967.JPG|thumb|"[[Love Is Here and Now You're Gone]]", "[[The Happening (song)|The Happening]]", and "[[Reflections (The Supremes song)|Reflections]]", all by [[The Supremes]] (''pictured''), appeared on the year-end list at numbers 26, 29, and 41, respectively.]]

[[File:The Rascals 1969.JPG|thumb|[[The Rascals|The Young Rascals]]' (''pictured'') three songs on the chart included "[[Groovin']]" (number nine), "[[I've Been Lonely Too Long]]" (number 54), "[[How Can I Be Sure]]" (number 63).]]
