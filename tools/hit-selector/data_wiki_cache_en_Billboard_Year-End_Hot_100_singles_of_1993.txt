[[File:Janet Tour.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Janet Jackson]] charted three songs from her 1993 album ''[[Janet (album)|Janet]]''—"[[That's the Way Love Goes (Janet Jackson song)|That's the Way Love Goes]]" at number four, "[[If (Janet Jackson song)|If]]" at number 19, and "[[Again (Janet Jackson song)|Again]]" at number 74.]]

[[File:Hi-Res NAACP DSC09523ACR (15317108755) (1).jpg|thumb|[[SWV]] (''pictured in 2014'') had three songs in the top-40 of the year-end chart: "[[Weak (SWV song)|Weak]]" (number 6), "[[I'm So Into You]]" (number 20), and "[[Right Here (SWV song)|Right Here (Human Nature)]]" (number 29).]]
