This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of 2006.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2006/hot-100-songs |title=Billboard Year End 2006 charts |website=[[Billboard.com]] |access-date=May 13, 2016}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|orig-date=December 23, 2006|title=Index of /Archive-All-Music/Billboard/00s/2006|url=https://worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Billboard/00s/2006/|url-status=live|access-date=2021-12-27|website=worldradiohistory.com|pages=80, 82|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211227003226/https://worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Billboard/00s/2006/ |archive-date=2021-12-27 }}</ref>

[[File:Daniel Powter.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Canadian singer-songwriter [[Daniel Powter]] topped the chart with his song "[[Bad Day (Daniel Powter song)|Bad Day]]."]]
[[File:The_Fray_2014.jpg|thumb|right|190px|American rock band [[The Fray (band)|The Fray]] had two songs on the chart, both in the top 30: "[[Over My Head (Cable Car)]]" at 13 and  "[[How to Save a Life (song)|How to Save a Life]]" at 27.]]
[[File:Chris Brown.jpg|thumb|right|190px| [[Chris Brown (American singer)|Chris Brown]] had four songs on the chart.  "[[Run It!]]" featuring [[Juelz Santana]] was his most successful song at 16.]]
[[File:NeYoJul05.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Ne-Yo]] had three songs on the chart from his debut album ''[[In My Own Words]]''. The most successful of these was "[[So Sick]]" at 17.]]
[[File:RihannaJingle Ball cropped.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] had two songs on the chart, both in the top 30: "[[SOS (Rihanna song)|SOS]]" at 19 and "[[Unfaithful (song)|Unfaithful]]" at 28.]]
[[File:Chad_Kroeger_(cropped).jpg|thumb|right|190px|Canadian rock band [[Nickelback]] had three songs on the chart from their album ''[[All the Right Reasons]]''. The most successful was "[[Photograph (Nickelback song)|Photograph]]" at 38.]]
[[File:T.I. by Carla.jpg|thumb|right|190px|American rapper [[T.I.]] had four songs on the chart,  "[[What You Know (T.I. song)|What You Know]]" was his most successful song at 47 on the chart. ]]
