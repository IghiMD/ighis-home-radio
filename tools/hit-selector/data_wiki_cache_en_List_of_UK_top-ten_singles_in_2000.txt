The [[UK Singles Chart]] is one of many music charts compiled by the [[Official Charts Company]] that calculates the best-selling singles of the week in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.theofficialcharts.com/ |title=The Official UK Charts Company |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |accessdate=1 January 2012}}</ref> Before 2004, the chart was only based on the sales of [[CD single|physical]] singles with [[airplay]] figures and [[Music download|digital downloads]] excluded from the official chart.<ref>{{cite book|title=[[Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums|Guinness World Records: British Hit Singles and Albums]]|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2005|isbn=1-904994-00-8|pages=14|edition=18th |publisher=[[HiT Entertainment|Guinness World Records Limited]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/music/3196892.stm |title=New singles formats to save the charts
|work=[[BBC News]]|date=16 October 2003 |accessdate=21 February 2010}}</ref> This list shows singles that peaked in the Top 10 of the [[UK Singles Chart]] during [[2000 in British music charts|2000]], as well as singles which peaked in [[List of UK top-ten singles in 1999|1999]] but were in the top 10 in 2000. The entry date is when the song appeared in the top 10 for the first time (week ending, as published by the Official Charts Company, which is six days after the chart is announced).

Two hundred and twenty-three singles were in the top ten in 2000. Thirteen singles from 1999 remained in the top 10 for several weeks at the beginning of the year. "[[Mr. Hankey, the Christmas Poo]]" by [[List of South Park characters#Mr. Hankey|Mr. Hankey]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/tv/ustv/feature/a662888/18-of-the-most-fascinating-south-park-facts-to-celebrate-its-18th-birthday/|title=18 of the most fascinating South Park facts to celebrate its 18th birthday|last=Eames|first=Tom|date=13 August 2015|work=Digital Spy|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> "[[Two in a Million]]"/"[[You're My Number One]]" by [[S Club 7]]<ref name = "Million">{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/hit-producers-stargate-have-signed-a-record-deal-and-have-teamed-up-with-pink-and-sia-for-their-debut-single-waterfall__18431/|title=Hit producers Stargate have signed a record deal and have teamed up with Pink and Sia for their debut single Waterfall|last=Copsey|first=Rob|date=8 March 2017|work=Official Chart|publisher=Official Charts Company|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and "[[Say You'll Be Mine (Steps song)|Say You'll Be Mine]]"/"[[Better the Devil You Know]]" by [[Steps (band)|Steps]]<ref name = "Steps">{{cite web|url=http://metro.co.uk/2017/03/14/steps-a-definitive-ranking-of-all-their-songs-from-worst-to-best-6506864/|title=Steps: A definitive ranking of all their songs from worst to best|last=O'Brien|first=Jon|date=14 March 2017|work=Metro|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> were the singles from 1999 to reach their peak in 2000. Forty-nine artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2000. [[Blink-182]], [[Coldplay]],
[[Nelly]], [[Pink (singer)|Pink]] and [[Sia]] were among the many artists who achieved their first UK charting top 10 single in 2000.

The 1999 [[List of Christmas number one singles (UK)|Christmas number-one]], "[[I Have a Dream (song)#Westlife version|I Have a Dream]]"/"[[Seasons in the Sun]]" by [[Westlife]], remained at number-one for the first three weeks of 2000.<ref name = "Seasons">{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-biggest-selling-singles-by-irish-acts-of-the-last-20-years__18498/|title=The Official Top 40 biggest selling singles by Irish acts of the last 20 years|last=Copsey|first=Rob|date=17 March 2017|work=Official Chart|publisher=Official Charts Company|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> The first new number-one single of the year was "[[The Masses Against the Classes]]" by Welsh band [[Manic Street Preachers]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://metro.co.uk/2016/03/17/15-songs-from-the-00s-you-probably-forgot-made-number-one-5747560/|title=15 songs from the 00s you probably forgot made number one|last=O'Brien|first=Jon|date=17 March 2016|work=Metro|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Overall, forty-three different singles peaked at number-one in 2000, with Westlife (4) having the most singles hit that position.<ref name = "Seasons" /><ref name = "Westlife number 1s">{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/3545399.stm|title=Boy band Westlife become foursome|date=9 March 2004|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref>

==Background==
===Multiple entries===
Two hundred and twenty-three singles charted in 2000, with 213 singles reaching their peak this year.

Forty-nine artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2000. British singer-songwriter and rapper [[Craig David]] and Irish group [[Westlife]] shared the record for the most top ten singles in 2000 with five entries. The most successful of these were "[[Fill Me In]]" and [[7 Days (Craig David song)|7 Days]]", which both reached the top of the chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-38400879|title=Craig David's had an amazing year - and it's 2016, not 2001|date=29 December 2016|work=Newsbeat|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|access-date=2 July 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2016/nov/04/mobos-craig-david-caps-comeback-year-with-best-male-award|title=Mobos: Craig David crowned best male act|last=Khomami|first=Nadia|date=5 November 2016|work=The Guardian|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> His other solo single that year, "[[Walking Away (Craig David song)|Walking Away]]" peaked two places lower at number 3.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2015/sep/14/craig-david-revival-comeback-bbc-blog|title=Re-re-rewind: is the Craig David revival upon us?|last=Gibsone|first=Harriet|date=14 September 2015|work=The Guardian|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> David had two hit singles in collaboration with [[Artful Dodger (UK band)|Artful Dodger]] - "[[Re-Rewind]]" charted at number 2 in the last weeks of 1999 and remained in the top ten throughout January 2000, while "[[Woman Trouble (song)|Woman Trouble]]" reached a high of number six.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/jun/01/re-rewind-the-odd-tale-of-how-uk-garage-legends-artful-dodger-became-original-dodger|title=Re-rewind! The odd tale of how UK garage legends Artful Dodger became Original Dodger|last=Muggs|first=Joe|date=1 June 2017|work=The Guardian|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Artful Dodger had two further top 10 singles without David, namely the number 2 hit "[[Movin' Too Fast]] (with [[Romina Johnson]]) and "[[Please Don't Turn Me On]]" featuring Lifford, peaking in fourth spot.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://mixmag.net/read/artful-dodger-return-as-original-dodger-news|title=Artful Dodger return as Original Dodger|date=6 May 2017|work=Mixmag|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref>

[[Westlife]] saw all but one of their singles in 2000 reach number-one, beginning with "[[I Have a Dream (song)#Westlife version|I Have a Dream]]"/"[[Seasons in the Sun]]", the 1999 Christmas number-one.<ref name = "Seasons" /> "[[Fool Again]]" repeated the feat in April,<ref name = "Westlife number 1s" /> and their duet with [[Mariah Carey]] - "[[Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)#Mariah Carey and Westlife version|Against All Odds]]" - did the same in September.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/live-reviews/review/a788067/mariah-carey-tour-live-review-the-divas-still-got-it/|title=Mariah Carey tour live review: Has Mimi still got it, or did she fall flat?|last=Corner|first=Lewis|date=24 March 2016|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> "[[My Love (Westlife song)|My Love]]" became their fourth chart topper of the year in November and "[[What Makes a Man]]" peaked one place lower in December 2000.<ref name = "Seasons" />

[[Britney Spears]], [[Eminem]], [[Kylie Minogue]], [[S Club 7]] and [[Steps (band)|Steps]] also had four hit singles in 2000. Spears had two number-one singles - "[[Born to Make You Happy]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/660983.stm|title=Britney Spears bubble gum deal|date=29 February 2000|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and "[[Oops!... I Did It Again (song)|Oops!... I Did It Again]]"<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/739948.stm|title=Britney does it again|date=7 May 2000|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> - among her top ten entries, which were rounded off by "[[Lucky (Britney Spears song)|Lucky]]" (number 5)<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/britney-spears-her-biggest-selling-singles-and-albums-revealed__9774/|title=Britney Spears: her biggest selling singles and albums revealed|last=Myers|first=Justin|date=13 August 2016|work=Official Chart|publisher=Official Charts Company|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and "[[Stronger (Britney Spears song)|Stronger]]" (7).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bustle.com/articles/195713-11-songs-that-were-popular-in-november-2000-that-you-need-to-revisit|title=11 Songs That Were Popular In November 2000 That You Need To Revisit|last=Lulic|first=Michelle|date=21 November 2016|work=Bustle|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Steps took "[[Stomp (Steps song)|Stomp]]" to the top of the charts<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/steps-biggest-selling-singles-revealed__18480/|title=Steps' biggest selling singles revealed|last=Copsey|first=Rob|date=14 March 2017|work=Official Chart|publisher=Official Charts Company|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and also appeared in the top ten with "[[Say You'll Be Mine (Steps song)|Say You'll Be Mine]]"/"[[Better the Devil You Know]]"<ref name = "Steps" /> and "[[Deeper Shade of Blue]]" (both number four) and "[[When I Said Goodbye / Summer of Love|When I Said Goodbye/Summer of Love]]", which reached number five.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/entry/steps-comeback-single-scared-of-the-dark-abba-new-album-tour-dates_uk_58bd0b4ee4b0b99894186683|title=Steps Confirm Comeback Single 'Scared Of The Dark', Album And Tour (All With A Little Help From ABBA)|last=Bagwell|first=Matt|date=6 March 2017|work=Huffington Post|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref>

[[Christina Aguilera]] achieved two more hit singles in 2000. Among her top ten entries where ”[[What a Girl Wants (Christina Aguilera song)|What a Girl Wants]]”(#3) and "[[Come On Over Baby (All I Want Is You)]]" (#8).

Pop group S Club 7's hit singles in 2000 included the chart-topping [[Children in Need]] single "[[Never Had a Dream Come True (S Club 7 song)|Never Had a Dream Come True]]",<ref name = "Dream">{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/2014/10/22/s-club-7-where-are-they-now_n_5492083.html|title=S Club 7 Reunion: Where Have The 'S Club Party' Singers Been Since The Band's Split?|last=McGrath|first=Rachel|date=22 October 2014|work=Huffington Post|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> number two entries "[[Reach (S Club 7 song)|Reach]]"<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/776081.stm|title=Sonique sees off Boyzone stars|date=4 June 2000|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and "[[Two in a Million]]"/"[[You're My Number One]]",<ref name = "Million" /> and "[[Natural (S Club 7 song)|Natural]] which peaked one place lower.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bustle.com/articles/165982-11-things-you-realize-about-s-club-7-when-you-listen-to-their-music-as-an|title=11 Things You Realize About S Club 7 When You Listen To Their Music As An Adult|last=Roberts|first=Amy|date=16 June 2016|work=Bustle|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Rapper Eminem had two number one singles - "[[The Real Slim Shady]]"<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2249738/eminem-real-slim-shady-vma-2000-performance/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150829055739/http://www.mtv.com/news/2249738/eminem-real-slim-shady-vma-2000-performance/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 29, 2015|title=VMAS: The Untold Story of How Eminem and 100 Slim Shadys Ruled the 2000 VMAs|last=Tardio|first=Andres|date=27 August 2015|work=MTV|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and the duet with [[Dido (singer)|Dido]], "[[Stan (song)|Stan]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.capitalxtra.com/artists/eminem/lists/best-songs/|title=20 of the best Eminem songs|date=15 June 2017|work=Capital Xtra|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> He also placed in the top ten with his [[Dr. Dre]] collaboration "[[Forgot About Dre]]"  (number seven)<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-100-biggest-hip-hop-songs-of-all-time__18789/|title=The Official Top 100 biggest Hip-Hop Songs of all time|last=Copsey|first=Rob|website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]] |date=17 April 2017|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> and solo single "[[The Way I Am (Eminem song)|The Way I Am]]" at number eight.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nouse.co.uk/2017/06/20/into-the-archives-eminem-the-marshall-mathers-lp/|title=Into The Archives: Eminem – The Marshall Mathers LP|last=Brown|first=Henry|date=20 June 2017|work=Nouse|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref>

Australian singer Minogue's biggest hit of the year was July's "[[Spinning Around]]" which spent a week at the top.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/music/4554139.stm|title=Pop star Kylie's route to success|date=17 May 2005|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Her other entries were "[[On a Night Like This]]", missing out on being her second number one of the year by one position, and "[[Please Stay (Kylie Minogue song)|Please Stay]]", a single week in tenth spot.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/930640.stm|title=Modjo holds Kylie from top spot|date=18 September 2000|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> Her fourth single to chart was "[[Kids (Robbie Williams and Kylie Minogue song)|Kids]]" at the end of the year with [[Robbie Williams]] - the song peaked at number one.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/sarah-sumeray/kids-robbie-williams-kylie-minogue_b_9661274.html|title=An Analysis of 'Kids' by Robbie Williams and Kylie Minogue - And Why I Think It Could Be the Greatest Song of the Century So Far|last=Sumeray|first=Sarah|date=12 April 2016|work=Huffington Post|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref>

[[Spice Girls]] member [[Melanie C]] was one of a number of artists with three top-ten entries, including number-one solo single "[[I Turn to You (Melanie C song)|I Turn to You]]" and "[[Holler (Spice Girls song)|Holler]]"/"[[Let Love Lead the Way]]" with the group, which also reached the top spot.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/1210127.stm|title=Mel C 'not quitting Spices'|date=8 March 2001|work=BBC News|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=2 July 2017}}</ref> [[A1 (band)|A1]], [[Destiny's Child]], [[Oasis (band)|Oasis]], [[Sonique (musician)|Sonique]] and [[Vengaboys]] were among the other artists who had multiple top 10 entries in 2000.

===Chart debuts===
Ninety-one artists achieved their first top 10 single in 2000, either as a lead or featured artist. Of these, eleven went on to record another hit single that year: [[Coldplay]], [[Darude]], [[Dr. Dre]], [[Madison Avenue (band)|Madison Avenue]], [[Oxide & Neutrino]], [[Pink (singer)|Pink]], [[Richard Blackwood]], [[Santana (band)|Santana]], [[Scooch]], [[Sisqó]] and [[True Steppers]]. [[Luck and Neat|DJ Luck & MC Neat]] and [[Sonique (musician)|Sonique]] both had two other entries in their breakthrough year.

==Entries by artist==
{{see also|List of UK Singles Chart number ones of the 2000s#2000}}
[[File:LeAnn Rimes on stage.jpg|thumb|right|150px|US country music singer [[LeAnn Rimes]] (pictured in 2010) reached number-one in November 2000 with "[[Can't Fight the Moonlight]]", the theme song from the movie ''[[Coyote Ugly]]''.]]
[[File:Melanie Chisholm byte.jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Melanie C]], known as Sporty Spice in the [[Spice Girls]], secured two solo entries this year, "[[I Turn to You (Melanie C song)|I Turn to You]]" and "[[Never Be the Same Again]]", which both spent a week at number-one. Her final single with the band, "[[Holler (Spice Girls song)|Holler]]"/"[[Let Love Lead the Way]]" also reached the top of the charts in November.]]
[[File:Queen And Adam Lambert - The O2 - Tuesday 12th December 2017 QueenO2121217-56 (26092186758).jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Brian May]] and [[Roger Taylor (Queen drummer)|Roger Taylor]] from [[Queen (band)|Queen]] (pictured in 2017) joined forces with boy band [[Five (band)|Five]] for a cover version of "[[We Will Rock You#Five + Queen version|We Will Rock You]]", which reached number-one in July 2000.]]
[[File:WithSMcropped.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Ireland's [[Samantha Mumba]] scored two top 10 hits during the year: "[[Gotta Tell You (song)|Gotta Tell You]]" peaked at number two while "[[Body II Body]]" reached number five.]]
[[File:Darude photo in 2015.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Finnish DJ [[Darude]] reached number three in June with his debut single and signature song "[[Sandstorm (Darude composition)|Sandstorm]]". His follow up single "[[Feel the Beat (Darude song)|Feel the Beat]]" also charted in the top 10, peaking at number five.]]
The following table shows artists who achieved two or more top 10 entries in 2000, including singles that reached their peak in 1999. The figures include both main artists and featured artists, while appearances on ensemble charity records are also counted for each artist. The total number of weeks an artist spent in the top ten in 2000 is also shown.
