[[File:Ace of Base Members 1992-2007.jpg|thumb|[[Ace of Base]]'s album [[The Sign (Ace of Base album)|''The Sign'']] had three songs in the top ten: "[[The Sign (song)|The Sign]]", "[[All That She Wants]]", and "[[Don't Turn Around]]".  The last time an album had such a strong showing was in [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1978|1978]], when [[Bee Gees]] recordings from the [[Saturday Night Fever (soundtrack)|''Saturday Night Fever'' soundtrack]] were in positions [[Night Fever|2]], [[Stayin' Alive|4]], and [[How Deep Is Your Love (Bee Gees song)|6]].]]
[[File:Mariah Carey3 Edwards Dec 1998 cropped.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Mariah Carey]] had the most songs on the year-end chart with four: "[[Hero (Mariah Carey song)|Hero]]" (number 5), "[[Without You (Badfinger song)#Mariah_Carey_version|Without You]]" (number 16), "[[Anytime You Need a Friend]]" (number 47), and "[[Endless Love (song)|Endless Love]]" (number 56), a duet with [[Luther Vandross]].]]
[[File:Tevin Campbell in 1996.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Tevin Campbell]] charted with the three songs "[[Can We Talk]]", "[[I'm Ready (Tevin Campbell song)|I'm Ready]]", and "[[Always in My Heart (Tevin Campbell song)|Always in My Heart]]" on 1995's year-end chart.]]
[[File:Janet Tour.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Janet Jackson]]'s year-end charting songs "[[Again (Janet Jackson song)|Again]]" (number 12), "[[Any Time, Any Place]]" (number 30), and "[[Because of Love]]" (number 48) all appeared on her 1993 album ''[[Janet (album)|Janet]]''.]]
[[File:KennethBabyfaceEdmondsHWOFMay2013.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Babyface (musician)|Babyface]] (''pictured in 2013'') had three songs on the Year-End Hot 100: "[[When Can I See You]]", "[[Never Keeping Secrets]]", and "[[And Our Feelings]]".]]
