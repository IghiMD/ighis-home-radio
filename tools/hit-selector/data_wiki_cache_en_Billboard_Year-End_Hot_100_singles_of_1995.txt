[[File:Coolio.jpg|thumb|right|190px|"[[Gangsta's Paradise]]" by [[Coolio]] (''pictured'') was the number one song on the Year-End chart after spending 12 weeks in the top-two of the Hot 100, three of which were spent at number one.]]
[[File:Boys II Men.jpg|thumb|right|[[Boyz II Men]] (''pictured'') had four songs on the Year-End chart, tying them with [[Brandy Norwood|Brandy]] and [[The Notorious B.I.G.]] for the most songs on the chart. These included the Hot 100 number one "[[On Bended Knee]]" at number five, "[[Water Runs Dry]]" at number 12, "[[I'll Make Love to You]]" at number 50, and "[[Thank You (Boyz II Men song)|Thank You]]" at number 94.]]
[[File:Brandy Essence Awards.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Brandy (''pictured'') tied for most songs on the Year-End chart with four, all of which were taken from her [[Brandy (album)|self-titled debut album]]: "[[Baby (Brandy Norwood song)|Baby]]" at number 37, "[[I Wanna Be Down]]" at number 49, "[[Brokenhearted (Brandy song)|Brokenhearted]]" at number 77, and "[[Best Friend (Brandy Norwood song)|Best Friend]]" at number 98.]]
[[File:Frank Hassas and Juergen Wind.jpg|thumb|right|Three songs by [[Real McCoy (band)|Real McCoy]], which consisted of producers Frank Hassas and Juergen Wind (''pictured''), were on the Year-End chart. Two were in the top-40 ("[[Another Night (Real McCoy song)|Another Night]]" at number six and "[[Run Away (Real McCoy song)|Run Away]]" at number 38) and their song "[[Come and Get Your Love#Real McCoy version|Come and Get Your Love]]" appeared at number 68.]]
[[File:Hootie and the Blowfish 1998 (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|[[Hootie & The Blowfish]]'s songs "[[Hold My Hand (Hootie & the Blowfish song)|Hold My Hand]]", "[[Let Her Cry (song)|Let Her Cry]]", and "[[Only Wanna Be with You]]" all appeared in the top-40 of the Year-End chart.]]
