{{Short description|Ranking of recorded music}}
[[File: Faith_Hill.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Faith Hill]]'s single "[[Breathe (Faith Hill song)|Breathe]]" was the first country music recording to be ranked number one since [[Johnny Horton]]'s "[[The Battle of New Orleans]]" in [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1959|1959]].  ([[Patsy Cline]]'s "[[I Fall to Pieces]]" ([[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1961|1961]]) and [[Glen Campbell]]'s "[[Rhinestone Cowboy]]" ([[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1975|1975]]) had each come close, ranking second.)  Her song "[[The Way You Love Me (Faith Hill song)|The Way You Love Me]]" also made the list, at 41.]]
[[File: Carlos Santana 2.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Latin rock band [[Santana (band)|Santana]] had a strong showing, taking positions two and three with "[[Smooth (Santana song)|Smooth]]" and "[[Maria Maria]]" from the ''[[Supernatural (Santana album)|Supernatural]]'' album.]]
