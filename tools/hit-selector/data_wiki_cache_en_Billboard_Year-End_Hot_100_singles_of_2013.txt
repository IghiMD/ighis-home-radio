The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. Throughout a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2013, the list was published on December 13, calculated with data from December 1, 2012, to November 30, 2013.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/how-we-charted-the-year-2013/|title=How We Charted the Year 2013|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=December 13, 2013|date=December 13, 2013|first= Silvio|last= Petroluongo}}</ref>

"[[Thrift Shop]]" by [[Macklemore]] and [[Ryan Lewis]] featuring [[Wanz]] topped the Year-End Hot 100 chart, with a total of 7,208,000 copies sold in the US.<ref name="yearend">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/macklemore-ryan-lewis-bruno-mars-highlight-year-end-hot-100/|title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis, Bruno Mars Highlight Year-End Hot 100 Chart|first1=Gary|last1=Trust|first2=Keith|last2=Caulfield|date=December 13, 2013|magazine=Billboard|access-date=December 25, 2013}}</ref><ref name="sales">{{cite web|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-justin-timberlake-paces-music-2013-212454294.html|title=Chart Watch: Justin Timberlake Paces The Year In Music 2013|first=Paul|last=Grein|date=December 18, 2013|publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]]|access-date=December 25, 2013}}</ref> Macklemore and Ryan Lewis's single "[[Can't Hold Us]]" featuring [[Ray Dalton]] also appeared in the list at number five, making the duo the fourth act to have two singles in the top five of the Year-End Hot 100 list.<ref name="yearend"/> The second best-selling single of 2013 was "[[Blurred Lines]]" by [[Robin Thicke]] featuring [[T.I.]] and [[Pharrell Williams|Pharrell]], having sold 6,380,000 copies in the country.<ref name="sales"/> Meanwhile, "[[Radioactive (Imagine Dragons song)|Radioactive]]" by [[Imagine Dragons]] and "[[Harlem Shake (song)|Harlem Shake]]" by [[Baauer]] was listed on the list at number three and four, respectively.
