[[File:Adele someone like you - cropped-2.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Adele]]'s "[[Rolling in the Deep]]" was the number 1 song of the year, topping the [[Hot 100]] for 7 consecutive weeks.]]
[[File:California Dreams Tour-Seattle.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Four of [[Katy Perry]]'s singles, "[[Firework (song)|Firework]]", "[[E.T. (song)|E.T.]]", "[[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)]]" and "[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry song)|Teenage Dream]]" managed to enter the list, with "Firework" and "E.T." being numbers 3 and 4 respectively.]]
[[File:Bruno Mars 2010.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Bruno Mars]]' single "[[Just the Way You Are (Bruno Mars song)|Just the Way You Are]]" jumped three spots from the previous year's list, from 18 to 15, but his song "[[Grenade (song)|Grenade]]" topped it at position 6. He also had "[[The Lazy Song]]" at 26 and was featured on "[[Lighters (song)|Lighters]]" at 34.]]
[[File:Nicki Minaj - Live Femme Fatale 9.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Nicki Minaj]]'s single "[[Super Bass]]" was number 8 on the list, as it peaked at number 3 on the Hot 100, she became the first female rapper to hit top 5 since [[Missy Elliott]]'s song "[[Lose Control (Missy Elliott song)|Lose Control]]".]]
[[File:Lil Wayne (23513397583).jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Lil Wayne]] has the most singles on the list with six including three lead singles from his album, ''[[Tha Carter IV]]'', with two singles "[[How to Love]]" and "[[6 Foot 7 Foot]]", ranking in the Top 50 at numbers 23 and 41 respectively, while "[[She Will]]" made it at number 95.]]
[[File:Rihanna, LOUD Tour, Belfast cropped 2.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Barbados|Barbadian]] singer [[Rihanna]] had five songs included on the list, four of which were from her fifth album, ''[[Loud (Rihanna album)|Loud]]'', and one from her sixth album, ''[[Talk That Talk (Rihanna album)|Talk That Talk]]''.]]
''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' publishes annual lists of songs based on chart performance over the course of a year based on [[Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems]] and [[Nielsen SoundScan|SoundScan]] information. For 2011, the list of the top 100 [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] [[Billboard Year-End|Year-End]] songs was published on December&nbsp;9, calculated with data from December&nbsp;4, 2010 to November&nbsp;26, 2011. At the number one position was [[Adele]]'s "[[Rolling in the Deep]]", which stayed atop the Hot 100 for seven consecutive weeks, and in the top thirty for most of the year.
