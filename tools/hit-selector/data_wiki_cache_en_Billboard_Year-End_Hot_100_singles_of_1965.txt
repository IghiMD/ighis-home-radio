This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] songs of [[1965 in music|1965]].<ref>"[https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-All-Music/Billboard/60s/1965/Billboard-1965-International-Record-&-Talent.pdf Top Singles of 1965]", ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] 1966 International Record & Talent Showcase''. Section Two. December 25, 1965. p. 23-24. Retrieved October 19, 2018.</ref> The Top 100, as revealed in the year-end edition of ''Billboard'' dated December 25, 1965, is based on Hot 100 charts from the issue dates of January 2 through October 30, 1965.

Turn! Turn! (To Everything There Is a Season)]]" and "[[Over and Over (Bobby Day song)|Over and Over]]". Plus the Top Tens "[[A Lover's Concerto]]", "[[Keep on Dancing (The Gentrys song)|Keep on Dancing]]", "[[Just a Little Bit Better]]", "[[Everybody Loves a Clown (song)|Everybody Loves a Clown]]", "[[Positively 4th Street]]", "[[You're the One (Petula Clark song)|You're the One]]" ([[the Vogues]]), "[[1-2-3 (Len Barry song)|1–2–3]]", "[[Rescue Me (Fontella Bass song)|Rescue Me]]", "[[A Taste of Honey (song)|A Taste of Honey]]", "[[Let's Hang On]]", "[[Ain't That Peculiar]]", "[[I Got You (I Feel Good)]]", "[[I Can Never Go Home Anymore]]", "[[England Swings]]", "[[Make the World Go Away]]" and "[[Fever (1956 song)|Fever]]" ([[the McCoys]]).
