[[File:Elton John with Luciano Pavarotti in Modena 1996 (2).jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Elton John]] (''pictured'') topped the Year-End chart with his double-sided single, "[[Candle in the Wind 1997]]" / "[[Something About the Way You Look Tonight]]", which was at the top of the Hot 100 for 14 weeks.]]
[[File:P Diddy 2000.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Sean Combs|Puff Daddy]] (''pictured'') had the most songs on the Year-End list with five: two of them, "[[I'll Be Missing You]]" and "[[Can't Nobody Hold Me Down]]", appeared in the top 10, and he was featured on three other songs  on the chart ("[[Mo Money Mo Problems]]", "[[No Time (Lil' Kim song)|No Time]]", and "[[Someone (SWV song)|Someone]]".)]]
[[File:Jewel at Clinton's inauguration party.jpg|thumb|right|190px|"[[You Were Meant for Me (Jewel song)|You Were Meant for Me]]" / "[[Foolish Games]]" by [[Jewel (singer)|Jewel]] (''pictured'') was number two on the Year-End list, despite never reaching the top of the Hot 100.]]

This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''{{'}}s Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of 1997.<ref>{{cite magazine|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|title=The Year In Music 1997 - Hot 100 Singles|date=December 27, 1997|volume=109|issue=52|url={{Google books|0g0EAAAAMBAJ|pg=SL680-PA32|plainurl=yes}}|page=YE-32|publisher=[[Nielsen Business Media, Inc.]]|issn=0006-2510|access-date=August 9, 2021}}</ref>

The list is also notable for featuring 14 songs that appeared in [[Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_1996|1996]]'s list, repeat onto to this list. With the highest being [[Toni Braxton]]'s "[[Un-Break My Heart]]", which barely made it on to 1996's list at number 81 only accounting six weeks of its run in the 1996 chart year, and repeat higher at number 4 in 1997's. Only four more year-end list would repeat the same feat, that being [[Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_2010|2010]], [[Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_2016|2016]], [[Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_2018|2018]] and [[Billboard_Year-End_Hot_100_singles_of_2022|2022]].
