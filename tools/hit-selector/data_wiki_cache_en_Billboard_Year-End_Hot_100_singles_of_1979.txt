[[File:Donna Summer (1977 Casablanca publicity headshot).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Donna Summer]] (''pictured'') had four songs on the year-end chart of 1979, the most of any artist that year: "[[Bad Girls (Donna Summer song)|Bad Girls]]" at number two, "[[Hot Stuff (Donna Summer song)|Hot Stuff]]" at number seven, "[[MacArthur Park (song)|MacArthur Park]]" at number 12, and "[[Heaven Knows (Donna Summer song)|Heaven Knows]]" at number 39.]]
[[File:Bee Gees 1977.JPG|thumb|upright|The [[Bee Gees]] had three songs within the top-40 of 1979's year-end chart: "[[Too Much Heaven]]" at number 11, "[[Tragedy (Bee Gees song)|Tragedy]]" at number 16, and "[[Love You Inside Out]]" at number 33.]]
