Her duet with [[Sam Fender]], "[[Rein Me In]]", later topped the chart for a record 23 non-consecutive weeks in 2026, becoming [[UK singles chart records and statistics#Most weeks at number one by single|the longest-running UK number-one single of all time]].]]

The [[UK Singles Chart]] is one of many music charts compiled by the [[Official Charts Company]] that calculates the best-selling singles of the week in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/|title=The Official Charts Company|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|accessdate=28 December 2014}}</ref> Since 2004 the chart has been based on the sales of both [[CD single|physical singles]] and [[music download|digital downloads]], with [[airplay]] figures excluded from the official chart.<ref>{{cite book|title=[[Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums|Guinness World Records: British Hit Singles and Albums]]|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2005|isbn=1-904994-00-8|pages=14|publisher=[[HiT Entertainment|Guinness World Records Limited]] |edition=18th }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/music/3196892.stm |title=New singles formats to save the charts
|work=[[BBC News]]|date=16 October 2003 |accessdate=21 February 2010}}</ref> Since 2014, the singles chart has been based on both sales and streaming, with the ratio altered in 2017 to 150:1 streams and only three singles by the same artist eligible for the chart.<ref name="Rules">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-40405357|title=Official Singles Chart rules are changing to help new bands break through|date=26 June 2017|work=Newsbeat|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|access-date=26 June 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicweek.com/talent/read/official-charts-company-changes-conversion-rate-to-reflect-rise-in-streaming/066932|title=Official Charts Company changes conversion rate to reflect rise in streaming|first=Ben|last=Homewood|date=19 December 2016|work=Music Week|accessdate=20 January 2017}}</ref> From July 2018, video streams from [[YouTube Music]] and [[Spotify]] among others began to be counted for the Official Charts. This list shows singles that peaked in the Top 10 of the [[UK Singles Chart]] during [[2025 in British music|2025]], as well as singles which peaked in [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2024|2024]] and [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2026|2026]] but were in the top 10 in 2025. The entry date is when the song appeared in the top 10 for the first time (week ending, as published by the Official Charts Company, which is six days after the chart is announced).

Ninety-three singles were in the top ten this year. Twenty-five singles from 2024 remained in the top 10 for several weeks at the beginning of the year, while "[[Step into Christmas]]" by [[Elton John]]", "[[Rein Me In]]" by [[Sam Fender]] and [[Olivia Dean]], "[[Where Is My Husband!]]" by [[Raye]], "[[So Easy (To Fall in Love)]]" by [[Olivia Dean]], "[[Opalite (song)|Opalite]]" by [[Taylor Swift]] and "[[Raindance (Dave song)|Raindance]]" by [[Dave (rapper)|Dave]] featuring [[Tems]] were all released in 2025 but did not reach their peak until 2026. "[[Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree]]" by [[Brenda Lee]] and "[[Fairytale of New York]]" by [[The Pogues]] featuring [[Kirsty MacColl]] charted in 2024 and re-entered the top 10 in 2025, but did not reach their peaks until 2026. "[[Not Like Us]]" by [[Kendrick Lamar]], "[[Luther (song)|Luther]]" by Kendrick Lamar and [[SZA]], "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]" by [[Mariah Carey]], "[[Jingle Bell Rock]]" by [[Bobby Helms]], "It Can't Be Christmas" by [[Tom Grennan]], "[[Santa Tell Me]]" by [[Ariana Grande]], "[[Underneath the Tree]]" by [[Kelly Clarkson]] and "[[Messy (Lola Young song)|Messy]]" by [[Lola Young (singer)|Lola Young]] were the songs from 2024 to reach their peak in 2025. [[Chrystal (musician)|Chrystal]], [[Alex Warren]], [[Doechii]], [[Ravyn Lenae]], [[Sombr]] and [[Olivia Dean]] were among the many artists who achieved their first top 10 single in 2025.

The 2024 [[List of Christmas number one singles (UK)|Christmas number-one]], "[[Last Christmas]]" by [[Wham!]], originally released in 1984, remained at number-one for the first week of 2025. The song later returned to number-one in December 2025, but was denied the Christmas number-one spot this time around by [[Kylie Minogue]]'s "[[XMAS (song)|XMAS]]". In the second week of 2025, [[Gracie Abrams]]' "[[That's So True]]", which had spent five weeks at number-one in 2024, returned to the top spot for a further three weeks. The first new number-one single of the year was "[[Messy (Lola Young song)|Messy]]" by [[Lola Young (singer)|Lola Young]]. Overall, thirteen different songs peaked at number-one in 2025, with [[Chappell Roan]] (2) having the most songs hit that position.

An asterisk (*) in the "Weeks in Top 10" column shows that the song is currently in the top 10.

==Background==

===Multiple entries===
Ninety-three singles charted in the top 10 in 2025 with seventy singles reaching their peak this year (including the re-entries "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]", "[[Jingle Bell Rock]]", "[[Santa Tell Me]]" and "[[Underneath the Tree]]", which charted in previous years but reached peaks on their latest chart run).

===Kendrick Lamar secures first UK number-one single following Super Bowl LIX performance===
On 14 February 2025 (20 February 2025, week ending), [[Kendrick Lamar]] re-entered the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]] simultaneously with "[[Not Like Us]]" and "[[Luther (song)|Luther]]" (a duet with [[SZA]]) following his performance at the [[Super Bowl LIX]] [[Super Bowl LIX halftime show|halftime show]]. The following week, Lamar secured his first UK number-one single when "Not Like Us" topped the charts, nine months after the song's original release, whilst his 2018 hit "[[All the Stars]]" (also a duet with SZA), re-entered the top-ten at its original peak of number 5.<ref>{{cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date= February 21, 2025|title=Kendrick Lamar earns first UK Number 1 single with Not Like Us| url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/kendrick-lamar-not-like-us-number-1-uk/|access-date=February 21, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Jack Black makes history with "Steve's Lava Chicken"===
On 2 May 2025 (8 May 2025, week ending), [[Jack Black]]'s "[[Steve's Lava Chicken]]" from the soundtrack of ''[[A Minecraft Movie]]'', running at just 34 seconds long, made history as the shortest song in duration to enter the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]] after it climbed to number nine.<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= May 2, 2025|title= Alex Warren makes it seven weeks at UK Singles Chart summit with Ordinary| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/alex-warren-ordinary-number-1-week-7/|access-date=May 2, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Alex Warren's "Ordinary" becomes longest-running UK number-one by US male solo artist===
On 6 June 2025 (12 June 2025, week ending), [[Alex Warren]]'s "[[Ordinary (Alex Warren song)|Ordinary]]" spent a twelfth consecutive week at number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]], becoming the longest-running UK number-one single ever by a US male solo artist, surpassing the eleven-week run at number-one in 1955 by [[Slim Whitman]]'s "[[Rose Marie (song)|Rose Marie]]", and the longest running number-one single since ACR was introduced in 2017.<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= June 6, 2025|title= Record Breaker! Alex Warren named US artist with longest-running UK Number 1 single ever! | url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/alex-warren-ordinary-record-breaker-longest-running-number-1-us-male-artist/ |access-date=June 6, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> After being knocked off the top spot the following week by [[Sabrina Carpenter]]'s "[[Manchild (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Manchild]]", "Ordinary" returned to number-one for a thirteenth non-consecutive week on 20 June 2025 (26 June 2025, week ending).<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= June 20, 2025|title= Alex Warren takes Ordinary back to the top | url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/alex-warren-ordinary-number-1-week-13/ |access-date=June 20, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Sabrina Carpenter's chart dominance continues as she secures fourth UK number-one single===
On 13 June 2025 (19 June 2025, week ending), [[Sabrina Carpenter]] secured her fourth number-one single with "[[Manchild (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Manchild]]", following her hat-trick of number one singles in 2024.<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= June 13, 2025|title= Sabrina Carpenter dethrones Alex Warren with fourth Number 1 single Manchild |url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-manchild-number-1-single/ |access-date=July 7, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The song topped the [[UK Singles Chart]] for two non-consecutive weeks and opened at the top spot with 62,400 units, the biggest opening week for a female artist in 2025 so far.<ref name="Smith">{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= July 4, 2025|title= Lewis Capaldi's Survive lands him his biggest opening week ever| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/lewis-capaldi-survive-number-1-single/ | access-date=July 4, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> In September 2025, Carpenter went on to secure the biggest opening week of 2025 for an international artist with the song's parent album ''[[Man's Best Friend (Sabrina Carpenter album)|Man's Best Friend]]'', opening with 85,500 chart units. This surpassed [[Lady Gaga]] with ''[[Mayhem (Lady Gaga album)|Mayhem]]'', which opened with 55,500 chart units in March 2025.<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=September 4, 2025|title= Sabrina Carpenter secures biggest opening week of 2025 for an international artist with Man's Best Friend| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-mans-best-friend-number-1-album/ | access-date=September 5, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> Carpenter was later surpassed by [[Taylor Swift]] in October 2025 with ''[[The Life of a Showgirl]]'', which opened with 423,000 units.<ref name=Taylor/>

===Lewis Capaldi earns biggest opening week of his career===
On 4 July 2025 (10 July 2025, week ending), [[Lewis Capaldi]] earned his sixth UK number-one single with "[[Survive (Lewis Capaldi song)|Survive]]", which debuted at the top of the [[UK Singles Chart]] in the wake of his performance at the [[Glastonbury Festival 2025]], which marked his first live appearance since 2023. The song opened with 68,500 units, earning Capaldi the biggest opening week of his career, as well as becoming the largest opening week of 2025 so far, surpassing "[[Manchild (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Manchild]]" by [[Sabrina Carpenter]].<ref name="Smith"/> In October 2025, Capaldi was later surpassed by [[Taylor Swift]]'s "[[The Fate of Ophelia]]" which opened with 132,000 units.<ref name=Taylor/>

===MK achieves his first UK number-one single thirty years after chart debut===
On 11 July 2025 (17 July 2025, week ending), [[MK (DJ)|MK]] achieved his first UK number-one single with "[[Dior (MK song)|Dior]]", thirty years after making his chart debut with "[[Always (MK song)|Always]]", which peaked at number 69 in August 1995. MK has become an artist with one of the longest gaps between their debut chart entry and chart-topper.<ref>{{cite web|last= White|first= Jack|date= July 11, 2025|title= Good things come to those who wait: the longest gaps between debut chart entries and first Number 1 single| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/good-things-come-to-those-who-wait-the-longest-gaps-between-debut-chart-entries-and-first-number-1s-on-the-official-singles-chart__29568/ | access-date=July 15, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The song also went on to become [[Chrystal (musician)|Chrystal]]'s first UK number-one single, and her second top-ten hit of the year, after "[[The Days (Chrystal song)|The Days]]".

===Justin Bieber secures his first UK number-one single in six years===
On 18 July 2025 (24 July 2025, week ending), [[Justin Bieber]] secured his 28th UK top-ten single with "[[Daisies (Justin Bieber song)|Daisies]]", which debuted at number four. The song reached number-one a week later on 25 July 2025 (31 July 2025, week ending), becoming Bieber's eighth UK number-one single, making it on the par with [[Sam Smith]], [[the Rolling Stones]] and [[Oasis (band)|Oasis]], and his first since "[[I Don't Care (Ed Sheeran and Justin Bieber song)|I Don't Care]]" (a duet with [[Ed Sheeran]]) topped the charts in May 2019.<ref>{{cite web|last= Levine|first= Nick|date= July 25, 2025|title= Justin Bieber lands eighth Number 1 single with DAISIES| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/justin-bieber-daisies-number-1-single/ | access-date=July 25, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===''KPop Demon Hunters'' dominates UK charts and sets records===
During 2025, four songs from [[KPop Demon Hunters (soundtrack)|the soundtrack]] of the [[Netflix]] animated film ''[[KPop Demon Hunters]]'' entered the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]]. Two of these, "[[Golden (Huntrix song)|Golden]]" (1) and "[[How It's Done (Huntrix song)|How It's Done]]" (9), were recorded by [[Huntrix]], a fictional K-pop girl group consisting of members Rumi, Mira, and Zoey (whose singing voices are performed by [[Ejae]], [[Audrey Nuna]], and [[Rei Ami]], respectively), whilst the other two, "[[Your Idol]]" (5) and "[[Soda Pop (song)|Soda Pop]]" (3), were recorded by [[KPop Demon Hunters|Saja Boys]], a fictional K-pop boy group consisting of members Jinu, Abs, Baby, Mystery and Romance (whose singing voices are performed by [[Andrew Choi]], Neckwav, Danny Chung, [[Kevin Woo]] and Samuil Lee, respectively).

On 1 August 2025 (7 August 2025, week ending), "Golden" reached number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]], becoming only the second-ever [[K-pop]] song to achieve that feat after "[[Gangnam Style]]" by [[Psy]] in 2012.<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= August 1, 2025|title= KPop Demon Hunters takeover! HUNTR/X's Golden becomes first K-pop Official Chart Number 1 in 13 years| url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/kpop-demon-hunters-huntrx-golden-number-1-single/ | access-date=August 8, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> After being knocked off the top spot the following week by [[Chappell Roan]]'s "[[The Subway (song)|The Subway]]",<ref name=ChappellAug8>{{cite web|last= Hill|first= Matt|date= August 8, 2025|title= Chappell Roan shuttles straight to Number 1 with The Subway|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/chappell-roan-shuttles-straight-to-number-1-with-the-subway/ | access-date=August 8, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> "Golden" returned to number-one on 15 August 2025 (21 August 2025, week ending),<ref>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= August 15, 2025|title= KPop Demon Hunters domination rages on as Golden reclaims top spot|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/kpop-demon-hunters-golden-number-1-week-2/| access-date=August 15, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> and went on to spend a total of ten non-consecutive weeks at the top spot.<ref name="Huntr/xNov7">{{cite web|last= Persad|first= Max|date= November 7, 2025|title= HUNTR/X make it 10 weeks at the top with Golden |url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/huntrx-golden-number-1-single-ten-weeks/| access-date=November 7, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

On 8 August 2025 (14 August 2025, week ending), "Golden", "Soda Pop" and "Your Idol" were placed at numbers two, six and eight, respectively, in the [[UK Singles Chart]], marking the first time that three K-pop songs charted in the top 10 of the UK chart simultaneously.<ref name=ChappellAug8/> On 19 September 2025 (25 September 2025, week ending), whilst "Golden" was spending its seventh non-consecutive week at number-one, "Soda Pop" and "Your Idol" were also placed in the top 5 at numbers three and five, respectively, which marked the first time that three K-pop songs charted in the top 5 of the UK chart simultaneously.<ref name="Huntr/xSep19">{{cite web|last= Persad|first= Max|date= September 19, 2025|title= Record Breaker! K-pop's going up, up, up as three KPop Demon Hunters tracks occupy Top 5 |url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/huntrx-golden-kpop-demon-hunters-top-5-record/ | access-date=September 19, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Olivia Dean secures Chart Double===
On 3 October 2025 (9 October 2025, week ending), [[Olivia Dean]] secured the coveted Chart Double after her single "[[Man I Need]]" climbed to number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]], whilst her album ''[[The Art of Loving (album)|The Art of Loving]]'' debuted at number-one in the [[UK Albums Chart]]. In doing so, Dean became the first British solo female artist to claim the Chart Double since Adele achieved it in 2021 with "[[Easy on Me]]" and the album ''[[30 (Adele album)|30]]''.<ref name="OliviaDean">{{cite web|last= Persad|first= Max|date= October 3, 2025|title= Dean does the Double! Man I Need and The Art of Loving claim the Official Chart Single and Album Number 1|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/dean-does-the-double-man-i-need-and-the-art-of-loving-claim-the-official-chart-single/| access-date=October 3, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Taylor Swift's chart records reach new peaks===
On 10 October 2025 (16 October 2025, week ending), [[Taylor Swift]] earned her fourth prestigious Chart Double after her single "[[The Fate of Ophelia]]" debuted at number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]], whilst the parent album ''[[The Life of a Showgirl]]'' debuted at number-one album in the [[UK Albums Chart]]. "The Fate of Ophelia" became Swift's fifth UK number-one single, as well as her fourth in the 2020s. Swift also attained the largest opening week for a single in 2025 so far, surpassing "[[Survive (Lewis Capaldi song)|Survive]]" by [[Lewis Capaldi]], with 132,000 units, becoming the largest first week sales for a single since December 2021. Additionally, Swift earned the highest first-week sales for an album in the 2020s with the parent album selling 423,000 units in its first week, which also became the highest first-week sales for an international artist in the 21st century.<ref name=Taylor>{{cite web|last= Kreisler|first= Lauren|date= October 10, 2025|title= Taylor Swift lands the biggest opening week of her career in the UK with The Life Of A Showgirl|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/taylor-swift-number-1-album-and-single/| access-date=October 10, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Lily Allen earns first top-ten single in eleven years===
On 7 November 2025 (13 November 2025, week ending), [[Lily Allen]] returned to the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]] with "[[Pussy Palace]]", which peaked at number 8. The song became Allen's first single to enter the UK top-ten since "[[Air Balloon (song)|Air Balloon]]" peaked at number 7 in March 2014.<ref name="Huntr/xNov7"/>

===Michael Jackson's "Thriller" reaches UK new chart peak amid Halloween surge===
On 7 November 2025 (13 November 2025, week ending), [[Michael Jackson]] re-entered the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]] with "[[Thriller (song)|Thriller]]" at number 9, surpassing the original peak at number 10 upon release in November 1983. The song re-entered the chart after becoming a popular Halloween standard.<ref name="Huntr/xNov7"/>

==="Last Christmas" returns to number-one, but is later denied Christmas number-one spot for the 3rd consecutive year by Kylie Minogue's XMAS===
On 12 December 2025 (18 December 2025, week ending), [[Wham!]]'s "[[Last Christmas]]" returned to number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]], notching an eleventh non-consecutive week at the top spot. This marked the fifth occasion the song reached number-one, having previously done so in January 2021, December 2022, December 2023 and December 2024. The song had also claimed the coveted [[List of UK singles chart Christmas number ones|Christmas number-one]] spot for two years in a row, in 2023 and 2024.<ref name=Wham!>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= December 12, 2025|title= WHAM!’s Last Christmas returns to the top of the Official Singles Chart|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/wham-last-christmas-number-1-single/| access-date=December 12, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> However, its chances of becoming the Christmas number-one single for the third successive year were dashed on 19 December 2025 (25 December 2025, week ending), as it descended to number two in the chart, whilst [[Kylie Minogue]]'s "[[XMAS (song)|XMAS]]" ascended to number-one and became 2025's [[List of UK singles chart Christmas number ones|Christmas number-one single]].<ref name=Kylie>{{cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= December 19, 2025|title= Christmas Number 1 2025: Kylie Minogue's XMAS breaks Official Chart records!|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/christmas-number-1-2025-kylie-minogue-xmas/| access-date=December 19, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> "Last Christmas" eventually claimed a twelfth non-consecutive week at number-one on 26 December 2025 (1 January 2026, week ending).<ref name=Wham!2>{{cite web|last= Persad|first= Max|date= December 26, 2025|title= Last Christmas regains Number 1 spot|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/wham-last-christmas-final-number-1-single-2025/| access-date=December 26, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Kylie Minogue secures the Christmas number-one with XMAS and sets records===
On 19 December 2025 (25 December 2025, week ending), [[Kylie Minogue]] secured her eighth UK number-one single with "[[XMAS (song)|XMAS]]", released exclusively on [[Amazon Music]], which became the [[List of UK singles chart Christmas number ones|Christmas number-one single of 2025]]. The song became Minogue's first chart-topper since "[[Slow (Kylie Minogue song)|Slow]]" topped the charts 22 years previously in November 2003, and Minogue was also the first female solo artist to earn a Christmas number-one since [[Sam Bailey]] reached number-one in December 2013 with "[[Skyscraper (song)#Sam Bailey version|Skyscraper]]". Minogue also set records by becoming the first female artist in UK Chart history to earn a number-one single across four decades, the 1980s, 1990s, 2000s and 2020s, as well as the oldest female solo artist to top the [[UK Singles Chart]] with new material.<ref name=Kylie/><ref name=YearinReview25>{{cite web|last= White|first= Jack|date= December 26, 2025|title= 2025 Music Year in Review: Reunions, record-breakers and a renaissance|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/2025-music-moments-biggest/ | access-date=December 26, 2025|publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

;Notes
[[Brian Eno]], [[Celeste (singer)|Celeste]], [[Leigh-Anne Pinnock]], [[London Community Gospel Choir]], [[Mabel (singer)|Mabel]] and [[Neneh Cherry]], who appear on "[[Together for Palestine#Charity single|Lullaby]]" by [[Together for Palestine]], had all previously reached the top-ten of the [[UK Singles Chart]], either as solo artists, as part of a group, or as part of a charity collective. Brian Eno produced multiple hit singles by numerous artists over his career, and, as one half of the side project Passengers with [[The Edge]] from U2, secured a number 6 hit in 1995 with "[[Miss Sarajevo]]". Celeste was a featured performer on the 2020 charity single "[[Times Like These (Foo Fighters song)#Live Lounge Allstars charity single|Times Like These]]" by [[Times Like These (Foo Fighters song)#Live Lounge Allstars charity single|Live Lounge Allstars]], which reached number-one. Leigh-Anne Pinnock enjoyed many top-ten singles as a member of [[Little Mix]], including five number-ones. London Community Gospel Choir were featured performers on the 2017 charity record "[[Bridge over Troubled Water (song)#Artists for Grenfell Tower charity single|Bridge Over Troubled Water]]" by [[Bridge over Troubled Water (song)#Artists for Grenfell Tower charity single|Artists for Grenfell]], which reached number-one. Mabel and Neneh Cherry both had multiple top-ten singles as solo artists.

=== Songs from films ===
Original songs from various films entered the top 10 throughout the year. These included "[[Defying Gravity (song)#Cynthia Erivo version|Defying Gravity]]" (from ''[[Wicked (2024 film)|Wicked]]''), "[[All the Stars]]" (from ''[[Black Panther (film)|Black Panther]]''), "[[Steve's Lava Chicken]]" (from ''[[A Minecraft Movie]]''), "[[Just Keep Watching]]" (from ''[[F1 (film)|F1]]'') and "[[Golden (Huntrix song)|Golden]]", "[[Your Idol]]", "[[Soda Pop (song)|Soda Pop]]" and "[[How It's Done (Huntrix song)|How It's Done]]" (all from ''[[KPop Demon Hunters]]'').

===Best-selling singles===
[[Alex Warren]] had the best-selling single of the year with "[[Ordinary (Alex Warren song)|Ordinary]]". The song spent thirteen weeks at number-one, sold 1,800,000 copies and was certified 3× platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry|BPI]]. "[[Messy (Lola Young song)|Messy]]" by [[Lola Young]] came in second place, while [[Chappell Roan]]'s "[[Pink Pony Club]]", [[Huntrix]]'s "[[Golden (Huntrix song)|Golden]]" and [[Bruno Mars]] and [[Rosé (singer)|Rosé]]'s "[[APT. (song)|APT.]]" made up the top five. Songs by [[Gracie Abrams]], [[Benson Boone]], [[Ravyn Lenae]], [[Olivia Dean]] and [[Lady Gaga]] and Bruno Mars were also in [[2025 in British music#Top singles of the year|the top ten best-selling singles of the year]].
