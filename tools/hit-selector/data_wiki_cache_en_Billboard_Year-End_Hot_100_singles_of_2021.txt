[[File:Dua Lipa with Warner Music.jpg|thumb|190px|"[[Levitating (song)|Levitating]]" by [[Dua Lipa]] ''(pictured)'' came in at number one in the Year-End list, despite peaking at number two on the weekly Hot 100 chart. It became the first song to top a Year-End list without topping the weekly chart since [[Lifehouse (band)|Lifehouse]]'s "[[Hanging by a Moment]]" in 2001.<ref name="yearend">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/dua-lipa-levitating-2021-hot-100-top-song-year-in-charts-1235004941/|title=The Year In Charts 2021: Dua Lipa's 'Levitating' Is the No. 1 Billboard Hot 100 Song of the Year|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|first1=Gary|last1=Trust|first2=Keith|last2=Caulfield|date=December 2, 2021|access-date=December 3, 2021}}</ref>]]
The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2021, the list was published on December 2, calculated with data from  November 21, 2020 to November 13, 2021.<ref name="yearend" />

''Billboard'' named [[Olivia Rodrigo]] the top Hot 100 artist of 2021,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2021/hot-100-artists/|title=Hot 100 Artists - Year-End 2021|year=2021|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 25, 2022}}</ref> the youngest female artist to achieve this honor, and the first female artist since [[Katy Perry]] in 2014.<ref name="yearend" /> Rodrigo placed four songs on the list, all in the top 40; the highest ranked of them, "[[Good 4 U]]", placed at number five.

==Year-end list==
[[File:The Weeknd with hand in the air performing live in Hong Kong in November 2018 (cropped).jpg|thumb|190px|[[The Weeknd]] ''(pictured)'' has four songs on the Year-End list, with "[[Save Your Tears]]" (with [[Ariana Grande]]) and "[[Blinding Lights]]" ranking at #2 and #3; in addition, "Blinding Lights", previously the biggest performing song of 2020, was crowned by ''Billboard'' as the most successful Hot 100 single of all time, dethroning Chubby Checker's "[[The Twist (song)|The Twist]]".<ref>{{Cite magazine|last=Mamo|first=Heran|date=2021-11-23|title=The Greatest Hit: The New No. 1 Song of All Time|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/features/the-weeknd-blinding-lights-billboard-cover-story-2021-interview-1235001282/|access-date=2021-12-03|magazine=Billboard|language=en-US}}</ref>]]

[[File:Olivia Rodrigo with Dr Fauci 2.png|thumb|190px|Four songs from [[Olivia Rodrigo]]'s ''(pictured)'' debut studio album ''[[Sour (album)|Sour]]'' rank within the top 40, with her number ones "[[Good 4 U]]" and "[[Drivers License (song)|Drivers License]]" ranking within the top 10 at number 5 and number 8 respectively, while "[[Deja Vu (Olivia Rodrigo song)|Deja Vu]]" and "[[Traitor (song)|Traitor]]" rank at number 13 and number 38 respectively.]]
