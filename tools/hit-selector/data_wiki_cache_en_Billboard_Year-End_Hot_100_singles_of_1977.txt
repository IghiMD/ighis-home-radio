This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of [[1977 in music|1977]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://longboredsurfer.com/charts/1977|title=Billboard Top 100 – 1977|access-date=2010-12-31}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|magazine=Billboard|date=December 24, 1977|title=Pop Singles of 1977|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=k0UEAAAAMBAJ&pg=PT61|access-date=September 16, 2014}}</ref> The Top 100, as revealed in the year-end edition of ''Billboard'' dated December 24, 1977, is based on Hot 100 charts from the issue dates of November 6, 1976 through October 29, 1977.
