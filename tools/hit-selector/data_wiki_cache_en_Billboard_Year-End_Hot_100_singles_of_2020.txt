[[File:The Weeknd (253662129).jpeg|thumb|190px|"[[Blinding Lights]]" by [[the Weeknd]] ''(pictured)'' was the best-performing single of 2020, spending a total of four nonconsecutive weeks at the top position of the Billboard Hot 100, as well as breaking Billboard records for both the most time ever spent in the Top 5, and the most spent in the Top 10.<ref name="yearend">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9493054/the-weeknd-blinding-lights-hot-100-song-2020-year-in-charts/|title=The Year in Charts 2020: The Weeknd's 'Blinding Lights' Is the No. 1 Hot 100 Song of the Year|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=December 3, 2020|access-date=December 4, 2020}}</ref>]]
The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2020, the list was published on December 3, calculated with data from  November 23, 2019, to November 14, 2020.<ref name="yearend"/>

''Billboard''{{'}}s top Hot 100 artist of 2020 was [[The Weeknd]],<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2020/hot-100-artists/|title=Hot 100 Artists - Year-End 2020|year=2020|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 25, 2022}}</ref> whose "[[Blinding Lights]]" was the number-one Hot 100 song of the year. It was one of two songs he placed on the list.

==Year-end list==
[[File:Roddy Ricch 2019.png|thumb|190px|[[Roddy Ricch]] ''(pictured)'' has six songs on the Year-End list, tying [[Lil Baby]] for the most songs on the list. Four of these songs are in the top 40, with his two highest-ranking songs being the number-one songs "[[The Box (Roddy Ricch song)|The Box]]" at number 3 and "[[Rockstar (DaBaby song)|Rockstar]]" ([[DaBaby]] featuring Roddy Ricch) at number 5.]]
[[File:Lil Baby - Openair Frauenfeld 2019 02.jpg|thumb|190px|[[Lil Baby]] ''(pictured)'' ties [[Roddy Ricch]] for the most songs on the list with six. Three songs from his second studio album ''[[My Turn (Lil Baby album)|My Turn]]'' land on the list, with "[[Woah (Lil Baby song)|Woah]]" at number 42, "[[Emotionally Scarred]]" at number 74, and "[[Sum 2 Prove]]" at number 79, in addition to two more from the album's deluxe edition, with "[[We Paid]]" (with [[42 Dugg]]) at number 57 and "[[The Bigger Picture (song)|The Bigger Picture]]" at number 76. He additionally is featured on a version of "[[Rags2Riches (song)|Rags2Riches]]"; however, that edition of the song was not listed.]]
[[File:DaBaby - The Know Show.png|thumb|190px|[[DaBaby]] ''(pictured)'' has five songs in the top 50, with his number-one song "[[Rockstar (DaBaby song)|Rockstar]]" (featuring [[Roddy Ricch]]) at number 5, "[[Whats Poppin]]" ([[Jack Harlow]] featuring DaBaby, [[Tory Lanez]], and [[Lil Wayne]]) at number 13, "[[Bop (DaBaby song)|Bop]]" at number 29, "[[My Oh My (Camila Cabello song)|My Oh My]]" ([[Camila Cabello]] featuring DaBaby) at number 37, and "[[For the Night]]" ([[Pop Smoke]] featuring [[Lil Baby]] and DaBaby) at number 49.]]
[[File:Drake July 2016.jpg|thumb|190px|[[Drake (musician)|Drake]] ''(pictured)'' has five songs on the list, with his highest-ranking being a feature on "[[Life Is Good (song)|Life Is Good]]" by [[Future (rapper)|Future]] at number 7.]]
[[File:Juice Wrld VMAs.png|thumb|190px|[[Juice Wrld]] ''(pictured)'' places five songs on the list, two of which are from his posthumously released third studio album ''[[Legends Never Die (Juice Wrld album)|Legends Never Die]]''. Those two songs are "[[Come & Go (Juice Wrld and Marshmello song)|Come & Go]]" (with [[Marshmello]]) and "[[Wishing Well (Juice Wrld song)|Wishing Well]]", which rank at number 54 and number 92 respectively.]]
