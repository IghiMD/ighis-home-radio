''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' publishes annual lists of songs based on chart performance over the course of a year based on [[Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems]] and [[Nielsen SoundScan|SoundScan]] information. For 2010, the list for the top 100 [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] [[Billboard Year-End|Year-End]] songs was published on December 8, calculated with data from December 5, 2009 to November 27, 2010. At the number-one position was [[Kesha]]'s "[[Tik Tok (song)|Tik Tok]]", which stayed atop the Hot 100 for nine weeks. This achievement made Kesha the first female artist in the history of the chart to top the Year-End Hot 100 with a debut single.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.biz/bbbiz/search/article_display.jsp?vnu_content_id=1004134049|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121002231803/http://www.billboard.biz/bbbiz/search/article_display.jsp?vnu_content_id=1004134049|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 2, 2012|title=Lady Gaga Is Billboard's 2010 Artist of the Year; Ke$ha Takes Top New Act|date=December 8, 2010|first=Keith|last=Caulfield|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=December 9, 2010}}</ref>

The list is one of five ''Billboard'' Year-End lists that featured 14 songs that appeared in the previous year (in this case [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2009|2009]]'s) repeat onto to this list. The highest being [[Jay Z]]'s "[[Empire State of Mind]]", which made it on to 2009's list at number 62 and repeat higher at number 21 in 2010's. Only four other year-end lists repeat the same feat, that being [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1997|1997]], [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2016|2016]], [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2018|2018]] and [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2022|2022]].
