[[File:Teddy Swims, 2021.jpg|thumb|190px|"[[Lose Control (Teddy Swims song)|Lose Control]]" by [[Teddy Swims]] ranked at number one on the Year-End list after spending a week atop the weekly Hot 100 chart in March.<ref name="yearend">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/hot-100-year-end-chart-teddy-swims-taylor-swift-republic/|title=Teddy Swims' 'Lose Control' Is 2024's No. 1 Hot 100 Song & Taylor Swift Is Chart's Top Artist|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=December 13, 2024|access-date=December 13, 2024}}</ref>]]
The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2024, the list was published on December 13, calculated with data from October 28, 2023, to October 19, 2024.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/2024-billboard-music-awards-finalists-full-list-1235837820/|title=Zach Bryan, Taylor Swift, Morgan Wallen & Sabrina Carpenter Are Top 2024 Billboard Music Awards Finalists: Full List|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|first=Paul|last=Grein|date=November 25, 2024|access-date=December 13, 2024}}</ref>

==Year-end list==
[[File:Morgan Wallen (cropped).jpg|thumb|190px|[[Morgan Wallen]] had five songs within the top 50, with "[[I Had Some Help]]" ([[Post Malone]] featuring Morgan Wallen) at number 4, "[[Last Night (Morgan Wallen song)|Last Night]]" at number 28, "[[Cowgirls (song)|Cowgirls]]" (featuring [[Ernest (singer)|Ernest]]) at number 29, "[[Thinkin' Bout Me]]" at number 31, and "[[Lies Lies Lies]]" at number 50.]]
[[File:Kendrick Lamar 2018 Pulitzer Prize ceremony (3x4 cropped).jpg |thumb|190px|Three of [[Kendrick Lamar]]'s diss tracks aimed at [[Drake (musician)|Drake]] made it on the Year-End list, with "[[Not Like Us]]" at number 6, "[[Like That (Future, Metro Boomin, and Kendrick Lamar song)|Like That]]" ([[Future (rapper)|Future]], [[Metro Boomin]] and Kendrick Lamar) at number 14, and "[[Euphoria (Kendrick Lamar song)|Euphoria]]" at number 66.]] 
[[File:Sabrina Carpenter - O2 Arena 2025 - 086 (cropped).jpg|thumb|190px|Four of [[Sabrina Carpenter]]'s songs entered the list, with "[[Espresso (song)|Espresso]]" at number 7, "[[Please Please Please (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Please Please Please]]" at number 16, "[[Feather (song)|Feather]]" at number 25, and "[[Taste (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Taste]]" at number 86.]]
[[File:Taylor Swift at the 2024 Golden Globes (2).png|thumb|285x285px|[[Taylor Swift]] notched five entries on the list, with "[[Cruel Summer (Taylor Swift song)|Cruel Summer]]" at number 12, "[[Fortnight (song)|Fortnight]]" (featuring [[Post Malone]]) at number 22, "[[Is It Over Now?]]" at number 33, "[[I Can Do It with a Broken Heart]]" at number 35, and "[[Down Bad (Taylor Swift song)|Down Bad]]" at number 99.]]
[[File:BillieEilishO2160622 (36 of 45) (52153469410) (cropped 2).jpg|thumb|190px|[[Billie Eilish]] placed four songs within the Year-End, three of which came from her third studio album ''[[Hit Me Hard and Soft]]'' as "[[Birds of a Feather (Billie Eilish song)|Birds of a Feather]]" ranks at number 15, "[[Lunch (song)|Lunch]]" ranks at number 48, and "[[Wildflower (Billie Eilish song)|Wildflower]]" ranks at number 89.]]
[[File:Drake in 2017.jpg|thumb|190px|[[Drake (musician)|Drake]] had four entries on the Year-End, with "[[Rich Baby Daddy]]" (featuring [[Sexyy Red]] and [[SZA]]) at number 45, "[[First Person Shooter (song)|First Person Shooter]]" (featuring [[J. Cole]]) at number 61, "[[Act II: Date @ 8]]" ([[4Batz]] and Drake) at number 80, and "[[IDGAF (Drake song)|IDGAF]]" (featuring [[Yeat]]) at number 83.]]
