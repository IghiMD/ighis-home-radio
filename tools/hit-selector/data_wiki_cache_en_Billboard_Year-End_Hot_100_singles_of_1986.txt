[[File:Madonna in concert wearing fishnets 1987 colorized.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Madonna]] charted three songs from her album ''[[True Blue (album)|True Blue]]'' on the year-end chart, two of which ("[[Papa Don't Preach]]" and "[[Live to Tell]]") appeared in its top-40.]]
[[File:Janet Jackson 1986 (A&M Publicity photo).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Janet Jackson]] had three songs on the year-end chart of 1986, all from her album ''[[Control (Janet Jackson album)|Control]]'': "[[When I Think of You]]" (number 32), "[[What Have You Done For Me Lately]]" (number 43), and "[[Nasty (Janet Jackson song)|Nasty]]" (number 58).]]
[[File:Whitney Houston - The Star-Ledger (1987).jpg|thumb|upright|"[[How Will I Know]]" and "[[The Greatest Love of All]]" by [[Whitney Houston]] (''pictured'') appeared at numbers six and 11, respectively, on the year-end chart.]]
[[File:Gloria Estefan Weezy Trish (27193732928) (cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Miami Sound Machine]], led by vocalist [[Gloria Estefan]] (''pictured''), had three songs on the year-end chart.]]

''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine each year releases a [[Billboard Year-End|Year-End]] chart of the most popular songs across all genres called the [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] songs of the year. This is the year-end Hot 100 songs of [[1986 in music|1986]].<ref>{{cite book |title=Billboard |date=27 December 1986 |publisher=Nielsen Business Media, Inc. |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=tiQEAAAAMBAJ&pg=RA1-PA9}}</ref>
