Michael Greene.jpg|thumb|German duo [[Milli Vanilli]] (''pictured, left and right'') had four songs in the top 30 of the chart from their studio album ''[[Girl You Know It's True (album)|Girl You Know It's True]]'': [[Girl You Know It's True (song)|its title track]], "[[Girl I'm Gonna Miss You]]", "[[Blame It on the Rain]]", and "[[Baby Don't Forget My Number]]".
