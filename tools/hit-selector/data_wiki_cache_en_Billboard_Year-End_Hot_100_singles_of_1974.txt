[[File:Barbra_Streisand_2_Allan_Warren_(cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|"[[The Way We Were (song)|The Way We Were]]" by [[Barbra Streisand]] was the number one song of 1974.]] 
[[File:Gladys Knight and the Pips on Soul Train.jpg|thumb|[[Gladys Knight and the Pips]] had three songs on 1974's year-end chart: "[[You're the Best Thing That Ever Happened to Me|Best Thing That Ever Happened to Me]]" (number 34), "[[I've Got to Use My Imagination]]" (number 41), and "[[On and On (Gladys Knight & the Pips song)|On and On]]" (number 69).]]
[[File:Paul and Linda McCartney (cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Paul McCartney]] (''pictured'') and his band [[Paul McCartney and Wings|Wings]] had one top-40 single on the year-end chart ("[[Band on the Run (song)|Band on the Run]]" at number 22) and two others on the chart ("[[Jet (song)|Jet]]" and "[[Helen Wheels]]".)]]
[[File:Jim Stafford 1975.JPG|thumb|upright|[[Jim Stafford]]'s song "[[Spiders & Snakes (song)|Spiders and Snakes]]" appeared at number 16 on the year-end chart; his songs "[[My Girl Bill]]" and "[[Wildwood Weed]]" also charted.]]
[[File:Terry Jacks 1974.JPG|thumb|[[Terry Jacks]]'s song "[[Seasons in the Sun]]" was the number-two song of 1974's year-end chart.]]
 
This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' singles of [[1974 in music|1974]].<ref>{{cite magazine |author=<!--Staff writer(s); no by-line.-->  |title=Top Pop Singles |url=https://worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Billboard/70s/1974/Billboard%201974-12-28-II-Talent-in-Action.pdf |magazine=Billboard Talent in Action |location=New York, New York |publisher=Billboard Publications, Inc. |page=8 |date=December 28, 1974 |access-date=December 6, 2020 }}</ref> The Top 100, as revealed in the Talent In Action section of ''Billboard'' dated December 28, 1974, is based on Hot 100 charts from the issue dates of November 24, 1973, through October 26, 1974.
