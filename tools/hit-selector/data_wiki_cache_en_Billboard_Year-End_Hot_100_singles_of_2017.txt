The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2017, the list was published on December 11, calculated with data from December 3, 2016 to November 25, 2017.<ref name="yearend" />

"[[Closer (The Chainsmokers song)|Closer]]" by [[The Chainsmokers]] featuring [[Halsey (singer)|Halsey]] was listed within the top 10 for the second year in a row, making it only the third song in history to achieve this, after [[Elton John]]'s "[[Candle in the Wind 1997]]" and [[LeAnn Rimes]]' "[[How Do I Live]]", both of which were ranked in the top ten of the [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1997|1997]] and [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 1998|1998]] lists.{{citation needed|date=January 2018}}

[[Ed Sheeran]] was named the top Hot 100 artist of 2017.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2017/hot-100-artists/|title=Hot 100 Artists - Year-End 2017|year=2017|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 25, 2022}}</ref> He scored the number-one Hot 100 song of the year with "[[Shape of You]]", one of two songs he placed on the list.

==Year-end list==

[[File:The Chainsmokers VELD 2016 (2).jpg|thumb|190px|Three songs from [[The Chainsmokers]] appear on the list, with "[[Something Just Like This]]" at number 5, "[[Closer (The Chainsmokers song)|Closer]]" at number 7 (second consecutive year in the top 10), and "[[Paris (The Chainsmokers song)|Paris]]" at number 42.]]
[[File:Migos Veld festival 2017.jpg|thumb|190px|American rap trio [[Migos]] charted five songs on the list with their number-one single "[[Bad and Boujee]]" featuring [[Lil Uzi Vert]] being the highest among them at number 6. One of its members, [[Quavo]], is also featured in three songs by other artists in the top 40.]]
[[File:Kendrick 2017.jpg|thumb|190px|American rapper [[Kendrick Lamar]] charted 4 songs on the list with the highest-ranking being his number-one single "[[Humble (song)|Humble]]" at number 4.]]
[[File:Alessia Cara performs for the 2017 Invictus Games opening ceremony(37023162840) (cropped).jpg|thumb|190px|Canadian singer [[Alessia Cara]] has three songs within the top 40, with "[[Stay (Zedd and Alessia Cara song)|Stay]]" (with [[Zedd]]) at number 17, "[[Scars to Your Beautiful]]" at number 30, and "[[1-800-273-8255 (song)|1-800-273-8255]]" (with [[Logic (rapper)|Logic]] and [[Khalid (American singer)|Khalid]]) at number 31.]]
[[File:OnedirectionWWATchile.jpg|thumb|190px|With the exception of [[Louis Tomlinson]], each member of the English-Irish boy band [[One Direction]] had at least one song on the list, with [[Zayn Malik|Zayn]] at number 26 with "[[I Don't Wanna Live Forever]]" (a duet with [[Taylor Swift]]), [[Niall Horan]] at number 32 with "[[Slow Hands (Niall Horan song)|Slow Hands]]", [[Liam Payne]] at number 36 with "[[Strip That Down]]" (featuring [[Quavo]]), and [[Harry Styles]] at number 87 with "[[Sign of the Times (Harry Styles song)|Sign of the Times]]".]]
