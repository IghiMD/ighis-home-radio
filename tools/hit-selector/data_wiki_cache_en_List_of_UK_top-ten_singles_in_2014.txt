[[File:Pharrell Williams - performing - summersonic - aug 16 2015.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Pharrell Williams]] had the best-selling single of 2014 with "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]", taken from [[Despicable Me 2#Music|the soundtrack]] of ''[[Despicable Me 2]]'', which first entered the top 10 in December 2013, peaked at number-one on 4 January 2014, and spent 20 weeks in the top 10 altogether.]]
[[File:John Legend 2019 by Glenn Francis.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[John Legend]] spent 19 non-consecutive weeks in the UK top 10 this year with "[[All of Me (John Legend song)|All of Me]]", which peaked at number two in April and became the third best selling single of the year.]]
The [[UK Singles Chart]] is one of many music charts compiled by the [[Official Charts Company]] that calculates the best-selling singles of the week in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/|title=The Official Charts Company|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|accessdate=28 December 2014}}</ref> Since 2004 the chart has been based on the sales of both [[CD single|physical singles]] and [[music download|digital downloads]], with [[airplay]] figures excluded from the official chart.<ref>{{cite book|title=[[Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums|Guinness World Records: British Hit Singles and Albums]] (18th edition)|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2005|isbn=1-904994-00-8|pages=14|publisher=[[HiT Entertainment|Guinness World Records Limited]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3196892.stm |title=New singles formats to save the charts
|work=[[BBC News]]|date=16 October 2003 |access-date=21 February 2010}}</ref> From 6 July, streaming figures became incorporated into the singles chart which means that a song will count as a sale, if streamed 100 times.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicweek.com/talent/read/official-charts-company-changes-conversion-rate-to-reflect-rise-in-streaming/066932|title=Official Charts Company changes conversion rate to reflect rise in streaming|first=Ben|last=Homewood|date=19 December 2016|work=Music Week|accessdate=20 January 2017}}</ref> This list shows singles that peaked in the Top 10 of the [[UK Singles Chart]] during [[2014 in British music charts|2014]], as well as singles which peaked in [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2013|2013]] but were in the top 10 in 2014. The entry date is when the single appeared in the top 10 for the first time (week ending, as published by the Official Charts Company, which is six days after the chart is announced).

One-hundred and fifty-five singles were in the top ten in 2014. Seven singles from 2013 remained in the top 10 for several weeks at the beginning of the year. "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]" by [[Pharrell Williams]]" (from the ''[[Despicable Me 2]]'' soundtrack) and "[[Trumpets (Jason Derulo song)|Trumpets]]" by [[Jason Derulo]] were the singles from 2013 to reach their peak in 2014. Fifty-eight artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2014. [[Ariana Grande]], [[Clean Bandit]], [[George Ezra]], [[Jess Glynne]], [[Meghan Trainor]] and [[Shawn Mendes]] were among the many artists who achieved their first UK charting top 10 single in 2014.

The first number-one single of the year was "Happy", which spent four non-consecutive weeks on top of the chart. The song vacated number-one for a week on 11 January 2014, being replaced by "[[Timber (Pitbull song)|Timber]]" by [[Kesha]] and [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]] before returning to the top spot. Overall, thirty-eight different singles peaked at number-one in 2014, with [[Ed Sheeran]] and [[Sam Smith (singer)|Sam Smith]] (3) having the joint most singles hit that position (including artist's participation on the [[Band Aid 30]] charity single).

==Background==
===Multiple entries===
One-hundred and fifty-two singles charted in the top 10 in 2014, with one-hundred and forty-three singles reaching their peak this year.

Fifty-eight artists scored multiple entries in the top 10 in 2014. [[Sam Smith (singer)|Sam Smith]] secured the record for most top 10 hits in 2014 with five hit singles.

===Chart debuts===
Ninety-four artists achieved their first top 10 single in 2014, either as a lead or featured artist. This includes the charity group [[Band Aid 30]] (made up of chart acts but charting together for the first time) and [[Gareth Malone]]'s [[Children in Need 2014|All Star Choir]]. Of these, ten went on to record another hit single that year: [[Ariana Grande]], [[Ella Henderson]], [[George Ezra]], [[Gorgon City]], [[Kiesza]], [[Melissa Steel]], [[Neon Jungle]], [[Robin Schulz]], [[Sam Martin (singer)|Sam Martin]] and [[Tiësto]]. [[5 Seconds of Summer]] and [[Clean Bandit]] achieved two more chart hits in 2014. [[Jess Glynne]] had three other entries in her breakthrough year.

;Notes
French Montana was featured on the East/West coast version of [[Chris Brown]]'s "[[Loyal (Chris Brown song)|Loyal]]" but not credited on the UK release. Becky Hill previously sang uncredited vocals on the [[Wilkinson (musician)|Wilkinson]] song "[[Afterglow (Wilkinson song)|Afterglow]]" in 2013. [[Clean Bandit]] officially had three singles as a group this year, but their members [[Grace Chatto]] and Jack and Luke Patterson featured on the [[Band Aid 30]] charity single "[[Do They Know It's Christmas?]]".

===Songs from films===
Original songs from various films entered the top 10 throughout the year. These included "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]" (from ''[[Despicable Me 2]]''), "[[Boom Clap]]" (''[[The Fault in Our Stars (film)|The Fault in Our Stars]]''), "[[Wasted (Tiësto song)|Wasted]]" (''[[22 Jump Street]]'') and "[[Beating Heart (Ellie Goulding song)|Beating Heart]]" (''[[Divergent (film)|Divergent]]'').

===Best-selling singles===
[[Pharrell Williams]] had the best-selling single of the year with "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]. The song spent 20 weeks in the top 10 (including four weeks at number one), sold around 1.85 million copies (including streams) and was certified 4× platinum by the [[British Phonographic Institute|BPI]]. "[[Rather Be (Clean Bandit song)|Rather Be]]" by [[Clean Bandit]] featuring [[Jess Glynne]]  came in second place, selling more than 1.52 million and losing out by around 330,000 sales. [[John Legend]]'s "[[All of Me (John Legend song)|All of Me]]", "[[Waves (Mr Probz song)|Waves]]" from [[Mr Probz]] and "[[Thinking Out Loud]]" by [[Ed Sheeran]] made up the top five. Singles by [[Ella Henderson]], [[Sam Smith (singer)|Sam Smith]], [[Meghan Trainor]], [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]] and [[George Ezra]] were also in the top 10 best-selling singles of 2014.

"Happy" also stands as the 9th [[List of best-selling singles in the United Kingdom|biggest-selling single of all time]] in the UK (as of December 2017).

==Entries by artist==
{{see also|List of UK Singles Chart number ones of the 2010s#2014}}
[[File:20150319174535!Sam Smith (Oct.jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Sam Smith]] had five UK top 10 singles in 2014, the most of any artist. These included the number-one solo hits "[[Money on My Mind]]" and "[[Stay with Me (Sam Smith song)|Stay with Me]]" (which became the year's fourth best seller), as well as a guest appearance on "[[Do They Know It's Christmas?]]" with [[Band Aid 30]].]]
[[File:Ed Sheeran at 2012 Frequency Festival in Austria (7852625324).jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Ed Sheeran]] had four UK top 10 singles this year. These included his number-one solo hits "[[Sing (Ed Sheeran song)|Sing]]" and "[[Thinking Out Loud]]" (which became the fifth best seller of 2014), as well as a guest appearance on "[[Do They Know It's Christmas?]]" with [[Band Aid 30]].]]
[[File:Ella Henderson (2) By Daniel Åhs Karlsson (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Ella Henderson]], who finished in sixth place in the [[The X Factor (British series 9)|ninth series of ''The X Factor'']], achieved two top 10 hits in 2014, including her debut single "[[Ghost (Ella Henderson song)|Ghost]]", which debuted at number-one in June and became the year's sixth best selling single.]]
[[File:Meghan Trainor in April 2015 (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|150px|[[Meghan Trainor]] made her UK top 10 debut in October of this year with "[[All About That Bass]]", which spent four weeks at number-one and became one of the biggest selling singles of the year.]]
[[File:Iggy Azalea 7-16-2014 -11 (14494841379).jpg|thumb|right|150px|Australian rapper [[Iggy Azalea]] scored three UK top singles in 2014, including "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]", a collaboration with American singer [[Ariana Grande]], which reached number-one for one week in July.]]
[[File:5 Seconds Of Summer (5SOS).jpg|thumb|right|150px|Another Australian act who made it big this year was [[5 Seconds of Summer]], who achieved three UK top 10 entries, including the number-one hit "[[She Looks So Perfect]]".]]
The following table shows artists who achieved two or more top 10 entries in 2014, including singles that reached their peak in 2013. The figures include both main artists and featured artists, while appearances on ensemble charity records are also counted for each artist. The total number of weeks an artist spent in the top ten in 2014 is also shown.
