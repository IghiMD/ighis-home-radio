[[File:Usher_2004_(cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|This year [[Usher (musician)|Usher]] took the top two spots with "[[Yeah! (Usher song)|Yeah!]]" and "[[Burn (Usher song)|Burn]]", plus the 12th and the 24th with "[[Confessions Part II]]" and "[[My Boo (Usher and Alicia Keys song)|My Boo]]", respectively. All four singles were from his [[Confessions (Usher album)|''Confessions'']]  album.]]
[[File:Alicia Keys at CES 2004.jpg|thumb|[[Alicia Keys]] had four songs, including "[[If I Ain't Got You]]", the number-three song of the year, on the year-end chart. "My Boo", her duet with Usher, "[[You Don't Know My Name]]", and "[[Diary (Alicia Keys song)|Diary]]" also all appeared within the top-40.]]
[[File:Ludacris.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Ludacris]] was featured on the number-one song of the year, Usher's "Yeah!", and [[Chingy]]'s song "[[Holidae In]]", which appeared at number 87. His songs "[[Splash Waterfalls]]" and "[[Stand Up (Ludacris song)|Stand Up]]" also appeared at numbers 42 and 45, respectively.]]
[[File:Kanye West in Portland.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Kanye West]] was featured on [[Twista]]'s number 16 song "[[Slow Jamz]]", while his songs "[[Jesus Walks (Kanye West song)|Jesus Walks]]" (number 43), "[[All Falls Down]]" (number 47) and "[[Through the Wire]]" (number 61) also all charted.]]
[[File:Beyoncé Knowles 2004 L'Oréal brand(cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Beyoncé]] had three songs on 2004's Year-End Hot 100: "[[Naughty Girl (Beyoncé Knowles song)|Naughty Girl]]" (number 18), "[[Me, Myself and I (Beyoncé Knowles song)|Me, Myself and I]]" (number 26), and "[[Baby Boy (Beyoncé Knowles song)|Baby Boy]]" (number 69). Her group [[Destiny's Child]] also charted at number 58 with the song "[[Lose My Breath]]".]]
[[File:OutKast 2001.jpg|thumb|upright|Hip-hop duo [[OutKast]] (''pictured in 2001'') had two songs in the top-10 of the 2004 year-end chart—"[[The Way You Move]]" at number five and "[[Hey Ya!]]" at number eight—as well as the song "[[Roses (Outkast song)|Roses]]" at number 56.]]
[[File:Chingy.jpg|thumb|[[Chingy]] charted with two songs as a lead artist on the year-end chart of 2004, including "[[One Call Away (Chingy song)|One Call Away]]" at number 25 and "Holidae In" at number 87, and was featured on the number 52 song of the year, "[[I Like That (Houston song)|I Like That]]" by [[Houston (singer)|Houston]].]]
