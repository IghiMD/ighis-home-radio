This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of 2007.<ref>[http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2007/hot-100-songs 2007 Year-End Hot 100 Songs]</ref>
[[File:Beyonce concert Barcelona.jpg|thumb|right|px|[[Beyoncé Knowles|Beyoncé]] topped the chart with her song "[[Irreplaceable]]" and reached number 62 with "[[Beautiful Liar]]," a collaboration with [[Shakira]].]]
[[File:Rihannalive2007.jpg|thumb|right|px|Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] had three songs on the chart, [[Umbrella (song)|Umbrella]]" at position 2 and  "[[Shut Up and Drive (Rihanna song)|Shut Up and Drive]]" at position 90 from her album ''[[Good Girl Gone Bad]]'', and  "[[Break It Off]]" at position 85 from her album ''[[A Girl Like Me (Rihanna album)|A Girl Like Me]].'']]
[[File:Akon 2008.jpg|thumb|right|px|[[Akon]] lent his vocals to six songs on the chart, four of which are in the top twenty.]]
[[File:Fergie 2007.jpg|thumb|right|px|American pop singer [[Fergie (singer)|Fergie]] had three songs on the chart, all in the top twenty. "[[Big Girls Don't Cry (Fergie song)|Big Girls Don't Cry]]" at 4, "[[Glamorous (Fergie song)|Glamorous]]" at 10, and  "[[Fergalicious]]" at 19.]]
[[File:PlainWhiteTs.jpg|thumb|right|px|[[Plain White T's|The Plain White T's]] had the most successful song on the chart by any band with  "[[Hey There Delilah]]" at position 7.]]
[[File:Justin Timberlake.jpg|thumb|right|px|Pop singer [[Justin Timberlake]] had the most songs on the chart, with seven.]]
