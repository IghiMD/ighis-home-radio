[[File: WKTU-FM's Miracle on 34th Street show - New York - seen on December 11, 1998 Cropped.jpg |thumb|right|190px|[[Cher]] (''pictured'') topped the list with "[[Believe (Cher song)|Believe]]" after the song was number one on the Hot 100 chart for four weeks, making her the oldest female artist to top the chart. It also gave her her first number one on the Hot 100 since "[[Dark Lady (song)|Dark Lady]]" in 1974, giving her the longest gap between number ones at nearly 25 years.]]
[[File:Whitney Houston 2000.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Whitney Houston]] (''pictured'') had four songs, all of which came from her 1998 album ''[[My Love Is Your Love]]'', on the Year-End chart, the most of any artist that year. These included "[[Heartbreak Hotel (Whitney Houston song)|Heartbreak Hotel]]" at number four, "[[It's Not Right but It's Okay]]" at number 44, "[[My Love Is Your Love (song)|My Love Is Your Love]]" at number 73, and "[[When You Believe]]" with [[Mariah Carey]] at number 99.]]
[[File:Mariah Carey 1999 (bigger).jpg|thumb|right|190px|"[[I Still Believe (Brenda K. Starr song)#Mariah Carey version|I Still Believe]]" and "When You Believe", two singles by [[Mariah Carey]] (''pictured'') from her 1998 compilation album ''[[Number 1's (Mariah Carey album)|#1's]]'', made it onto the Year-End list at numbers 36 and 99, respectively, while her song "[[Heartbreaker (Mariah Carey song)|Heartbreaker]]" placed at number 35.]]
[[File:Faith Evans 1998 (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|190px|Two singles by [[Faith Evans]] (''pictured'') from her 1998 album ''[[Keep the Faith (Faith Evans album)|Keep the Faith]]''—"[[Love Like This (Faith Evans song)|Love Like This]]" and "[[All Night Long (Faith Evans song)|All Night Long]]"—appeared on the Year-End list at numbers 58 and 74, respectively. Evans was also featured on Houston's "Heartbreak Hotel" alongside [[Kelly Price]].]]
[[File:Shania Twain - Come on Over Tour - Boston.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Shania Twain]] had three songs on 1999's year-end chart: "[[That Don't Impress Me Much]]" at number 32, "[[From This Moment On (Shania Twain song)|From This Moment On]]" at number 57, and "[[Man! I Feel Like a Woman!]]" at number 77. All of them appeared on her 1997 studio album ''[[Come On Over]]''.]]
[[File:NSYNC members 2000s.png|thumb|[[NSYNC]] appeared thrice on the 1999 year-end chart. Their song "[[(God Must Have Spent) A Little More Time on You]]" charted at both number 45 and at number 100, the latter being a collaboration with [[Alabama (band)|Alabama]], and their song "[[Music of My Heart]]" with [[Gloria Estefan]] charted at number 97.]]
[[File:Jay-Z-01-mika (cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Jay-Z]]'s three appearances on the year-end list include his feature on Carey's song "[[Heartbreaker (Mariah Carey song)|Heartbreaker]]", his highest entry at number 35, as well as his songs "[[Can I Get A...]]" (number 39) and "[[Hard Knock Life (Ghetto Anthem)]]" (number 89).]]
