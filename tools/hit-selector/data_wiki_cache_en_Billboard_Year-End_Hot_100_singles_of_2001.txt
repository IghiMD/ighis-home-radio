This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of 2001.<ref>{{cite magazine|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|title=The Year In Music 2001 - Hot 100 Singles & Tracks|date=December 29, 2001|volume=113|issue=52|url={{Google books|sBIEAAAAMBAJ|pg=RA1-PA16|plainurl=yes}}|page=YE-38|publisher=[[Nielsen Business Media, Inc.]]|issn=0006-2510|access-date=August 10, 2021}}</ref>
[[File:Lifehouse_2007.jpg|thumb|"[[Hanging by a Moment]]" by [[Lifehouse (band)|Lifehouse]] (''pictured in 2007'') was the best-performing single of 2001.]]
[[File:Destiny_Child_at_Super_Bowl_XLVII_halftime_show.jpg|thumb|[[Destiny's Child]] (''pictured in 2013'') placed three songs on the list. The highest was their 11-week number-one song "[[Independent Women Part I]]", ranking at number 10.]]
[[File:JenniferLopez.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Jennifer Lopez]] (''pictured in 2004'') had the number-five song of 2001 with "[[I'm Real (Jennifer Lopez song)|I'm Real]] (Murder Remix)" and two other songs—"[[Love Don't Cost a Thing (song)|Love Don't Cost a Thing]]" at number 26 and "[[Play (Jennifer Lopez song)|Play]]" at number 72.]]
[[File:Nelly.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Nelly]] (''pictured in 2007'') had three songs on the year-end chart of 2001: "[[Where the Party At]]" with [[Jagged Edge (American group)|Jagged Edge]], "[[Ride wit Me]]" (featuring [[City Spud]]), and his solo song "[[E.I. (song)|E.I.]]".]]
[[File:Alicia Keyes (5112509447) (cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Alicia Keys]]'s song "[[Fallin' (Alicia Keys song)|Fallin']]" appeared at number two on the year-end chart for 2001.]]
