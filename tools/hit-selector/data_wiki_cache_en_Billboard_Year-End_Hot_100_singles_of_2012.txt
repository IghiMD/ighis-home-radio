[[File:Gotye, 2012.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Gotye]]'s single, "[[Somebody That I Used to Know]]", came in at number one, spending a total of 8 weeks at number one throughout the year.]]
[[File:Carly Rae Jepson-DSC 0065-10.20.12 (8107368763).jpg|thumb|right|upright|Singer [[Carly Rae Jepsen]], whose single "[[Call Me Maybe]]" appeared at number 2, is the highest-ranking solo song by a female artist on the list.]]
[[File:Ellie Goulding at Manchester Academy 2012 - Portrait.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Singer [[Ellie Goulding]], whose single "[[Lights (Ellie Goulding song)|Lights]]" appeared at number 5, is the highest-ranking British artist on the list.]]
[[File:Rihanna 5, 2012.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]]'s "[[We Found Love]]" came in at number 69 in 2011 and rose to position 8 this year. She had a total of six songs included on the list, with her as a lead artist on 5 songs. Four of which were from her sixth album ''[[Talk That Talk (Rihanna album)|Talk That Talk]]'', and one was from her seventh album ''[[Unapologetic (album)|Unapologetic]]''.]]
[[File:Flo Rida2012.jpg|thumb|right|190px|All of rapper [[Flo Rida]]'s singles off of ''[[Wild Ones (Flo Rida album)|Wild Ones]]'' are within the top 20, except "[[I Cry (Flo Rida song)|I Cry]]", which was not on the Year-End chart but peaked at #6, and would then proceed to be on 2013's Year-End chart, and "[[Let It Roll (Flo Rida song)|Let It Roll]]", which did not chart on the Hot 100 at all.]]
[[File:Wiz Khalifa High Road.jpg|thumb|right|190px|American rapper [[Wiz Khalifa]]'s feature on [[Maroon 5]]'s single, "[[Payphone (song)|Payphone]]" came in at number 4 on the list. His singles, "[[Young, Wild & Free]]" with [[Snoop Dogg]] and [[Bruno Mars]], and "[[Work Hard, Play Hard]]" charted at number 32 and number 73 respectively, while his feature on [[T-Pain]]'s single, "[[5 O'Clock (T-Pain song)|5 O'Clock]]" made it at number 86.]]
[[File:Katy Perry UNICEF 2012.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Three of [[Katy Perry]]'s singles from ''[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry album)|Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection]]'' are in the top 50, with "[[Wide Awake (Katy Perry song)|Wide Awake]]" at number 15, "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]" at number 31, and "[[The One That Got Away (Katy Perry song)|The One That Got Away]]" at number 41.]]
''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' publishes annual lists of songs based on chart performance over the course of a year based on [[Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems]] and [[Nielsen SoundScan|SoundScan]] information. For 2012, the list for the top 100 [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] [[Billboard Year-End|Year-End]] songs was published on December&nbsp;14, calculated with data from December&nbsp;3, 2011 to November 24, 2012.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1481472/the-best-of-2012-the-year-in-music?page=0%2C1|title=The Best of 2012: The Year In Music|year=2012|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=December 8, 2012}}</ref> At the number-one position was [[Gotye]]'s "[[Somebody That I Used to Know]]" featuring [[Kimbra]], which stayed atop the Hot 100 for eight consecutive weeks.
