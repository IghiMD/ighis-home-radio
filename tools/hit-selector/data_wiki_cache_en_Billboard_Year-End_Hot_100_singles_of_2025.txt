The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information. For 2025, the list was published on December 9, calculated with data from October 26, 2024, to October 18, 2025.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/lady-gaga-bruno-mars-2025-year-end-billboard-hot-100/|title=Lady Gaga & Bruno Mars' 'Die With a Smile' Makes History as Year-End Hot 100 No. 1|work=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=December 9, 2025|access-date=December 9, 2025}}</ref>

Four songs on this list ("[[A Bar Song (Tipsy)]]", "[[Lose Control (Teddy Swims song)|Lose Control]]", "[[Beautiful Things (Benson Boone song)|Beautiful Things]]" and "[[I Had Some Help]]") were listed within the top 10 for the second year in a row, making 2025 the year with the most songs to achieve this feat.
