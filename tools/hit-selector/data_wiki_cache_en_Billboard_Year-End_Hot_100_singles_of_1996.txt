[[File:Mariah Carey in Holland, April 1 1998 cropped.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Mariah Carey]] (''pictured'') had three songs on the Year-End chart from her album ''[[Daydream (Mariah Carey album)|Daydream]]'': the Hot 100 number ones "[[One Sweet Day]]" with [[Boyz II Men]] and "[[Always Be My Baby]]" at numbers two and five, respectively, and "[[Fantasy (Mariah Carey song)|Fantasy]]" at number 49.]]
[[File:LL.COOL J. 1999.jpg|thumb|right|190px|All three singles from the 1995 album ''[[Mr. Smith (album)|Mr. Smith]]'' by [[LL Cool J]] (''pictured'') were featured on the Year-End chart, with two—"[[Hey Lover]]" and "[[Loungin]]"—appearing in the top-40.]]
[[File:Hootie and the Blowfish 1998 (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Hootie & the Blowfish]] (''pictured'') charted with three songs: "[[Time (Hootie & the Blowfish song)|Time]]" at number 50, "[[Old Man & Me (When I Get to Heaven)]]" at number 74, and "[[Only Wanna Be with You]]" at number 99.]]
[[File:Celine Dion by Linda Bisset.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Celine Dion]]'s (''pictured'') Hot 100 number one single "[[Because You Loved Me]]" charted at number three on the Year-End chart, while her song "[[It's All Coming Back to Me Now]]" peaked at number 18.]]
