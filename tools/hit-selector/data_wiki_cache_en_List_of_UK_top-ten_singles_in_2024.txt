The [[UK Singles Chart]] is one of many music charts compiled by the [[Official Charts Company]] that calculates the best-selling singles of the week in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/|title=The Official Charts Company|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|accessdate=28 December 2014}}</ref> Since 2004 the chart has been based on the sales of both [[CD single|physical singles]] and [[music download|digital downloads]], with [[airplay]] figures excluded from the official chart.<ref>{{cite book|title=[[Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums|Guinness World Records: British Hit Singles and Albums]]|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2005|isbn=1-904994-00-8|pages=14|publisher=[[HiT Entertainment|Guinness World Records Limited]] |edition=18th }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3196892.stm |title=New singles formats to save the charts
|work=[[BBC News]]|date=16 October 2003 |access-date=21 February 2010}}</ref> Since 2014, the singles chart has been based on both sales and streaming, with the ratio altered in 2017 to 150:1 streams and only three singles by the same artist eligible for the chart.<ref name="Rules">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-40405357|title=Official Singles Chart rules are changing to help new bands break through|date=26 June 2017|work=Newsbeat|publisher=British Broadcasting Corporation|access-date=26 June 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicweek.com/talent/read/official-charts-company-changes-conversion-rate-to-reflect-rise-in-streaming/066932|title=Official Charts Company changes conversion rate to reflect rise in streaming|first=Ben|last=Homewood|date=19 December 2016|work=Music Week|accessdate=20 January 2017}}</ref> From July 2018, video streams from [[YouTube Music]] and [[Spotify]] among others began to be counted for the Official Charts. This list shows singles that peaked in the Top 10 of the [[UK Singles Chart]] during [[2024 in British music|2024]], as well as singles which peaked in [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2023|2023]] and [[List of UK top-ten singles in 2025|2025]] but were in the top 10 in 2024. The entry date is when the song appeared in the top 10 for the first time (week ending, as published by the Official Charts Company, which is six days after the chart is announced).

One hundred singles were in the top ten this year. Fourteen singles from 2023 remained in the top 10 for several weeks at the beginning of the year, while "[[Not Like Us]]" by [[Kendrick Lamar]], "[[Luther (song)|Luther]]" by Kendrick Lamar and [[SZA]], "[[Jingle Bell Rock]]" by [[Bobby Helms]], "It Can't Be Christmas" by [[Tom Grennan]], "[[Underneath the Tree]]" by [[Kelly Clarkson]] and "[[Messy (Lola Young song)|Messy]]" by [[Lola Young (singer)|Lola Young]] were all released in 2024 but did not reach their peak until 2025. "[[Stick Season (song)|Stick Season]]" by [[Noah Kahan]], "[[Merry Christmas (song)|Merry Christmas]]" by [[Ed Sheeran]] and [[Elton John]] and "[[It's Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas#Michael Bublé version|It's Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas]]" by [[Michael Bublé]] were the songs from 2023 to reach their peak in 2024. "[[Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree]]" by [[Brenda Lee]] charted in 2023 and re-entered the top 10 in 2024, but did not reach its peak until 2025. [[Teddy Swims]], [[Benson Boone]], [[Artemas (musician)|Artemas]], [[Dasha (singer)|Dasha]], [[Sabrina Carpenter]], [[Chappell Roan]] and [[Gracie Abrams]] were among the many artists who achieved their first top 10 single in 2024.

Country music dominated the [[UK Singles Chart]] during the first half of 2024, with an unprecedented six country music songs entering the top ten in the space of four months, with "[[Texas Hold 'Em (song)|Texas Hold 'Em]]" by [[Beyoncé]] being the highest-charting of these, reaching number-one.

The 2023 [[List of Christmas number one singles (UK)|Christmas number-one]], "[[Last Christmas]]" by [[Wham!]], originally released in 1984, remained at number-one for the first week of 2024. The song later returned to number-one in December 2024, and became the Christmas number-one for the second year in a row. The first new number-one single of the year was "[[Stick Season (song)|Stick Season]]" by [[Noah Kahan]]. Overall, thirteen different songs peaked at number-one in 2024, with Sabrina Carpenter (3) having the most songs hit that position.

==Background==
===Multiple entries===
Ninety-nine singles charted in the top 10 in 2024, with eighty-six singles reaching their peak this year (including the re-entries "[[Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree]]", "[[Merry Christmas (song)|Merry Christmas]]" and "[[It's Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas#Michael Bublé version|It's Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas]]", which charted in previous years but reached peaks on their latest chart run).

==="Murder on the Dancefloor" returns to the top-ten after use in Saltburn===
On 5 January 2024 (11 January 2024, week ending), [[Sophie Ellis-Bextor]] returned to the UK top-ten for the first time since 2007, when her 2001 hit "[[Murder on the Dancefloor]]" re-entered the chart at number 8. The song, which originally peaked at number 2 upon its initial release, witnessed a resurgence in popularity following its use in the film ''[[Saltburn (film)|Saltburn]]''. The song also experienced its best ever streaming week, earning 293,000 weekly streams.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=January 5, 2024|title=The Saltburn Effect! Sophie Ellis-Bextor on Murder on the Dancefloor's Top 10 return: "Thank you for this magical adventure!"|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sophie-ellis-bextor-murder-on-the-dancefloor-top-10-biggest-streaming-week-saltburn/|access-date=January 5, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> "Murder on the Dancefloor" reclaimed its 2001 peak of number 2 the following week, and surpassed the previous week's best for Ellis-Bextor by earning the most one-week streams since its release, with 4.7 million UK streams over the previous seven days.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=January 12, 2024|title= Sophie Ellis-Bextor’s Murder on the Dancefloor reclaims UK chart peak amid Saltburn resurgence|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sophie-ellis-bextor-murder-on-the-dancefloor-saltburn-repeak/|access-date=January 12, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Madonna secures first UK top 10 single in fifteen years===
On 2 February 2024 (8 February 2024, week ending), [[Madonna (entertainer)|Madonna]] secured her first UK top 10 single since "[[Celebration (Madonna song)|Celebration]]" in September 2009, thanks to her inclusion on "[[Popular (The Weeknd, Playboi Carti and Madonna song)|Popular]]", also featuring [[The Weeknd]] and [[Playboi Carti]], after the song climbed to number 10 in the chart, having spent a total of 12 non-consecutive weeks inside the top 40 before finally breaking into the upper echelons of the chart. Madonna also earned her 64th top 10 hit in the process, extending her record as the female artist with the most UK top 10 singles.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=February 2, 2024|title= Madonna celebrates landmark 64th UK Top 10 single, and first in 15 years|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/madonna-celebrates-landmark-64th-uk-top-10-single-and-first-in-15-years/|access-date=February 2, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Noah Kahan claims coveted Chart Double===
On 16 February 2024 (22 February 2024, week ending), [[Noah Kahan]] claimed the coveted Chart Double after his album ''[[Stick Season (album)|Stick Season]]'' climbed to number-one in the [[UK Albums Chart]] while [[Stick Season (song)|the album's title track of the same name]] spent its seventh consecutive week at number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=February 16, 2024|title= The season of Noah Kahan! ===Beyoncé earns first UK number-one single in fourteen years and later secures second Chart Double===
On 23 February 2024 (29 February 2024, week ending), [[Beyoncé]] rose to the top of the UK Singles Chart with "[[Texas Hold 'Em (song)|Texas Hold 'Em]]" after debuting at number 9 the week before. The song became Beyoncé's fifth UK number-one as a solo artist (eighth overall) and her first number-one single in fourteen years, since "[[Telephone (song)|Telephone]]" (a collaboration with [[Lady Gaga]]) topped the charts in March 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=February 23, 2024|title= Beyoncé’s TEXAS HOLD ‘EM lassoes itself to Number 1 – becomes superstar’s first UK chart-topper in 14 years|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/beyonce-texas-hold-em-uk-number-1/|access-date=February 23, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The song initially spent four non-consecutive weeks at the top of the chart, becoming Beyoncé's longest-running UK number-one single to date,<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=March 15, 2024|title= TEXAS HOLD ‘EM becomes Beyoncé’s longest-running UK Number 1 single|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/beyonce-texas-hold-em-number-1-week-4-ariana-grande/|access-date=March 15, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> before returning to number-one for a fifth non-consecutive week on 5 April 2024 (11 April 2024, week ending), after the album ''[[Cowboy Carter]]'' debuted at the top of the [[UK Albums Chart]], marking the second time that Beyoncé had secured the UK Chart Double.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=April 5, 2024|title= Beyoncé is first black artist ever to take country album to Number 1 in the UK|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/beyonce-official-chart-double-cowboy-carter-texas-hold-em-number-1/ |access-date=April 5, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Taylor Swift's chart dominance continues as she secures her third Chart Double===
[[Taylor Swift]]'s dominance of the [[UK Singles Chart]] continued into 2024. On 26 April 2024 (2 May 2024, week ending), three tracks from her album ''[[The Tortured Poets Department]]'' debuted inside the top 5 of the chart, including her duet with [[Post Malone]], "[[Fortnight (song)|Fortnight]]", which became her fourth UK number-one single, whilst ''The Tortured Poets Department'' debuted at number-one in the [[UK Albums Chart]], thus giving Swift her third Chart Double. "Fortnight" also became Malone's first number-one single in almost seven years, since "[[Rockstar (Post Malone song)|Rockstar]]" topped the charts in 2017.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=April 26, 2024|title= Taylor Swift’s The Tortured Poets Department secures UK’s biggest opening week in seven years|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/taylor-swift-tortured-poets-department-fortnight-uk-number-1-chart-double/ |access-date=April 26, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> On 3 May 2024 (9 May 2024, week ending), a fourth track from ''The Tortured Poets Department'', "[[I Can Do It with a Broken Heart]]", entered the top 10 at number 8.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=May 3, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter gets major caffeine hit! Espresso is her first UK Number 1 single|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-espresso-number-1-song-uk/ |access-date=May 3, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Eminem achieves eleventh UK number-one single===
On 7 June 2024 (13 June 2024, week ending), [[Eminem]]'s "[[Houdini (Eminem song)|Houdini]]" debuted at the top of the [[UK Singles Chart]], becoming his eleventh number-one single in the United Kingdom, making it on the par with [[Calvin Harris]]. During its first week of release, the single accumulated 104,800 chart units, including 13.3 million streams, the biggest opening week for a single of 2024 so far.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=June 7, 2024|title= Eminem nets biggest opening week of 2024 so far as Houdini hits Number 1|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/eminem-houdini-number-1-song/ |access-date=June 7, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The song remained at number-one the following week, and became Eminem's first single to spend two weeks at the top of the UK charts.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=June 14, 2024|title= Eminem hits personal best with Houdini's second week at Number 1|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/eminem-houdini-number-1-week-2-sabrina-carpenter-please-please-please/ |access-date=June 14, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Sabrina Carpenter sets records===
On 21 June 2024 (27 June 2024, week ending), [[Sabrina Carpenter]], at 25 years, 1 month, 1 week and 3 days old, became the youngest female artist to occupy the number one and number two spots in the [[UK Singles Chart]] simultaneously, after "[[Please Please Please (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Please Please Please]]" climbed to the top spot whilst her former chart-topper "[[Espresso (song)|Espresso]]" held at the runner-up position. "Please Please Please" was also the UK's most-streamed song of the week, totalling 9.8 million combined streams, whilst "Espresso" came second, with 8.1 million.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=June 21, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter makes UK Official Chart history as Please Please Please claims Number 1|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-please-please-please-espresso-record-breaker/ |access-date=June 21, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> On 5 July 2024 (11 July 2024, week ending), Carpenter set yet another record by becoming the first-ever female artist to secure three consecutive weeks at number one and number two spots in the UK Singles Chart concurrently. She also became only the second act to achieve this feat after [[The Beatles]], who achieved the same feat on two occasions, managing three consecutive weeks at numbers one and two in 1963 with "[[I Want to Hold Your Hand]]" and "[[She Loves You]]" and again in 1967 with "[[Hello Goodbye]]" and ''[[Magical Mystery Tour|Magical Mystery Tour (EP)]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=July 5, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter becomes first female artist in chart history to secure three consecutive weeks at Numbers 1 and 2 concurrently|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-please-please-please-espresso/ |access-date=July 5, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> She extended her record to four consecutive weeks on 12 July 2024 (18 July 2024, week ending), whilst "Please Please Please" was dethroned from the summit and "Espresso" rebounded to number-one for a sixth non-consecutive week,<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=July 12, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter replaces herself at Number 1 as Espresso returns to the top|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-espresso-returns-to-number-1/ |access-date=July 12, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> then set another record on 19 July 2024 (25 July 2024, week ending), when she became the first-ever female artist to occupy the top two spots of the UK chart for five consecutive weeks, and only the second to ever do so, after [[Ed Sheeran]].<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=July 19, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter makes history (again!) as Espresso hits Number 1 for a seventh week|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-espresso-number-1-week-7/ |access-date=July 19, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

On 30 August 2024 (5 September 2024, week ending), Carpenter made history yet again by becoming the first-ever female artist to occupy the entire top three of the [[UK Singles Chart]], after "[[Taste (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Taste]]" debuted at number-one, becoming her third consecutive chart-topper, whilst "Please Please Please" and "Espresso" climbed back to numbers two and three, respectively. Carpenter also earned her first UK Chart Double as the parent album ''[[Short n' Sweet]]'' simultaneously debuted at number one on the [[UK Albums Chart]] with 89,000 chart sales, the second highest first week sales for an album in 2024.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date= August 30, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter scores record-breaking Official Chart Double with Short n' Sweet and Taste|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-official-chart-double-short-n-sweet-taste-oasis/| access-date=August 30, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> Carpenter set yet another record on 6 September 2024 (12 September 2024, week ending), when she became the first female artist ever to occupy the top three spots of the chart concurrently for two consecutive weeks.<ref name=6Sep>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date= September 6, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter's Taste spends second week at Number 1 as Oasis take Live Forever to new highs|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-official-chart-double-short-n-sweet-taste-oasis/| access-date=September 6, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> On 20 September 2024 (26 September 2024, week ending), during "Taste"'s fourth week at the top spot, Carpenter made history again by becoming the female solo artist with the most combined weeks at number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]] in a calendar year, with 16 in total,<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date= September 20, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter's Taste reigns supreme at Number 1 - and makes UK chart history|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-taste-number-1-week-4/| access-date=September 20, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> eventually extending her record to 21.<ref name="SabrinaOct25">{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date= October 25, 2024|title= Sabrina Carpenter smashes ninth consecutive week at Number 1 with Taste|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sabrina-carpenter-taste-number-1-week-9/ | access-date=October 25, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Charli XCX earns first number-one single in eleven years===
On 9 August 2024 (15 August 2024, week ending), [[Charli XCX]] debuted at the top of the [[UK Singles Chart]] with "[[Guess (song)|Guess]]" which became her second UK number-one single, and first in eleven years since "[[I Love It (Icona Pop song)|I Love It]]" (with [[Icona Pop]]) reached the summit in 2013. The song also includes an appearance by [[Billie Eilish]], who secured her third UK number-one single following 2020's "[[No Time to Die (song)|No Time to Die]]" and 2023's "[[What Was I Made For?]]".<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=August 9, 2024|title= Charli xcx and Billie Eilish debut at Number 1 with Guess remix|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/charli-xcx-billie-eilish-guess-number-1-single/ |access-date=August 9, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Chase & Status secure first-ever number-one single===
On 16 August 2024 (22 August 2024, week ending), "[[Backbone (Chase & Status and Stormzy song)|Backbone]]", a collaboration by [[Chase & Status]] and [[Stormzy]], debuted at the top of the [[UK Singles Chart]], and became the first UK number-one single for Chase & Status and the fourth for Stormzy. "Backbone" was the most-streamed track in the UK during its first week of release, accumulating 6.4 million streams.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=August 16, 2024|title= Chase & Status and Stormzy’s BACKBONE debuts at Number 1: "It couldn't get any better for us" |url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/chase-and-status-stormzy-backbone-number-1-single/ |access-date=August 16, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

===Oasis singles re-enter top-ten simultaneously after reunion announcement===
In August 2024, 15 years after their initial disbandment, [[Britpop]] legends [[Oasis (band)|Oasis]] announced that they would reform for performances in the UK and Ireland in July and August 2025 during their [[Oasis Live '25 Tour]]. In the wake of the announcement, sales and streams of their back catalogue increased significantly.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Smith|first= Carl|date=August 30, 2024|title= Oasis reunion: Definitely Maybe, (What's The Story) Morning Glory? and Time Flies albums surge|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/oasis-reunion-definitely-maybe-time-flies-whats-the-story-morning-glory/|access-date=August 30, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> On 6 September 2024 (12 September 2024, week ending), "[[Live Forever (Oasis song)|Live Forever]]" and "[[Don't Look Back in Anger]]" re-entered the UK top-ten simultaneously, with "Live Forever" reaching a brand new peak of number 8, surpassing its original peak of number 10 in 1994.<ref name=6Sep/>

==="Last Christmas" claims Christmas number-one for the second year in a row===
On 13 December 2024 (19 December 2024, week ending), [[Wham!]]'s "[[Last Christmas]]" returned to number-one in the [[UK Singles Chart]] for an eighth non-consecutive week, 40 years after its initial release. The song had previously topped the chart in 2020 (for one week), 2022 (for two non-consecutive weeks), and 2023 (for four consecutive weeks) and had claimed the coveted [[List of UK singles chart Christmas number ones|Christmas number-one spot]] for the first time in its history on 22 December 2023 (28 December 2023, week ending).<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=December 13, 2024|title= WHAM! return to Number 1 with Last Christmas as Tom Grennan & Kelly Clarkson enter Top 10|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/wham-last-christmas-number-1-return/|access-date=December 13, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The following week, the song claimed the Christmas number-one spot for the second year in a row, and became the first-ever single in the chart's 72-year history to clinch two consecutive Christmas number-one victories.<ref>{{Cite web|last= Griffiths|first= George|date=December 20, 2024|title= Christmas Number 1 2024: WHAM!'s Last Christmas makes Official Chart history again!|url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/christmas-number-1-2024-whams-last-christmas-makes-official-chart-history-again-andrew-ridgeley/|access-date=December 20, 2024|website=Official Charts Company}}</ref>

;Notes
[[¥$]] was an American hip hop supergroup composed of rapper [[Kanye West]] and singer [[Ty Dolla Sign]], both of whom had UK top 10 singles, either as solo artists or as part of collaborations with other artists. [[Perrie Edwards|Perrie]] and [[Jade Thirlwall|Jade]] both earned their first UK top 10 singles as solo artists in 2024 with "[[Forget About Us]]" and "[[Angel of My Dreams]]". These also became their first top 10 entries since the UK girl band [[Little Mix]] went on their hiatus in May 2022.

===Songs from films===
The only song from a film which entered the top 10 this year was "[[Defying Gravity (song)#Cynthia Erivo version|Defying Gravity]]" (from ''[[Wicked (2024 film)|Wicked]]'').

===Best-selling singles===
[[Noah Kahan]] had the best-selling single of the year with "[[Stick Season (song)|Stick Season]]". The song spent seven weeks at number-one, sold 2,400,000 copies and was certified 4× platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry|BPI]]. "[[Beautiful Things (Benson Boone song)|Beautiful Things]]" by [[Benson Boone]] came in second place, while [[Sabrina Carpenter]]'s "[[Espresso (song)|Espresso]]", [[Teddy Swims]]' "[[Lose Control (Teddy Swims song)|Lose Control]]" and [[Hozier]]'s "[[Too Sweet]]" made up the top five. Songs by [[Shaboozey]], [[Billie Eilish]], [[Chappell Roan]], [[Dasha (singer)|Dasha]] and [[Sabrina Carpenter]] ("[[Please Please Please (Sabrina Carpenter song)|Please Please Please]]") were also in [[2024 in British music#Top singles of the year|the top ten best-selling singles of the year]].

*{{note label|Cruel Summer|A|a}} "Cruel Summer" re-entered the top 10 at number 3 on 2 November 2023 (week ending) following the release of a live version to tie in with the cinematic release of the film ''[[Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour]]''.
*{{note label|Cruel Summer|B|b}} "Cruel Summer" re-entered the top 10 at number 7 on 16 November 2023 (week ending).
*{{note label|Last|C|c}} "Last Christmas" re-entered the top 10 at number 5 on 7 December 2023 (week ending). The song originally peaked at number 2 upon its initial release in 1984 and reached number-one for the first time ever on 7 January 2021 (week ending).
*{{note label|All|D|d}} "All I Want for Christmas Is You" re-entered the top 10 at number 6 on 7 December 2023 (week ending). The song originally peaked at number 2 upon its initial release in 1994 and reached number-one for the first time ever on 17 December 2020 (week ending).
*{{note label|Fairytale|E|e}} "Fairytale of New York" re-entered the top 10 at number 4 on 14 December 2023 (week ending), following the death of [[The Pogues]]' lead singer [[Shane MacGowan]]. The song originally peaked at number 2 upon release in 1987.
*{{note label|Rockin'|F|f}} "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" re-entered the top 10 at number 6 on 14 December 2023 (week ending). Having originally peaked at number 6 upon its original release in 1962, the song reached a new peak of number 4 on 5 January 2023 (week ending).
*{{note label|Merry|G|g}} "Merry Christmas" re-entered the top 10 at number 7 on 14 December 2023 (week ending), having originally peaked at number-one upon release in 2021.
