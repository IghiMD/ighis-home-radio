[[File:50_Cent_(Cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[50 Cent]]'s (''pictured in 2006'') "[[In da Club]]" took the top spot, one of four singles from his debut studio album ''[[Get Rich or Die Tryin']]'' to make the list (at positions [[21 Questions|14]], [[P.I.M.P.|21]], and [[Wanksta|63]]).  He was also featured on [[Lil' Kim]]'s "[[Magic Stick]]", ranked 20.]]
[[File:R. Kelly mug shot.jpg|thumb|upright|[[R. Kelly]] had the number-two song of 2003 ("[[Ignition (Remix)]]") as well as two other songs on the year-end chart ("[[Thoia Thoing]]" at number 61 and "[[Step in the Name of Love]]" at number 94.)]]
[[File:Jay-Z-02-mika.jpg|thumb|upright|Two of [[Jay-Z]]'s songs as a lead artist, "[['03 Bonnie & Clyde]]" and "[[Excuse Me Miss]]", appeared at numbers 37 and 54 on 2003's year-end chart. He was also featured on "[[Crazy in Love]]" (number four) and "[[Frontin']]" (number 34).]]
[[File:Beyonce (132337561).jpeg|thumb|upright|[[Beyoncé]] had two songs in the top-15 of the year-end chart for 2003: "Crazy in Love" at number four and "[[Baby Boy (Beyoncé Knowles song)|Baby Boy]]" at number 12. She was also featured on Jay-Z's song "'03 Bonnie & Clyde", which appeared at number 37.]]
[[File:EMINEM rapping Anger management tour 2003 (cropped).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Eminem]] had three songs on 2003's Year-End Hot 100: "[[Lose Yourself]]" at number 28, "[[Sing for the Moment]]" at number 89, and "[[Superman (Eminem song)|Superman]]" at number 98.]]
[[File:Stripped Tour - Latin Medley - Christina Aguilera.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Christina Aguilera]]'s songs "[[Beautiful (Christina Aguilera song)|Beautiful]]", "[[Can't Hold Us Down]]", and "[[Fighter (Christina Aguilera song)|Fighter]]" all charted on 2003's year-end chart.]]
[[File:Ashanti at WrestleMania XIX.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Ashanti (singer)|Ashanti]]'s songs "[[Rock wit U (Awww Baby)]]" (number 29) and "[[Rain on Me (Ashanti song)|Rain on Me]]" (number 83) both appeared on 2003's Year-End Hot 100. She was also featured on [[Ja Rule]]'s song "[[Mesmerize (Ja Rule song)|Mesmerize]]", which charted at number 25.]]

This is a list of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine's Top '''Hot 100''' songs of 2003.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=2003 The Year in Music: Hot 100 Singles & Tracks|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=bA8EAAAAMBAJ&pg=RA1-PA5|access-date=February 11, 2017|magazine=Billboard|date=December 27, 2003|pages=YE-30, YE-32}}</ref>

The list is also notable for only three songs appearing in the list from [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2002|2002]]. In contrast, as many as nine also appeared in the list from [[Billboard Year-End Hot 100 singles of 2004|2004]].
