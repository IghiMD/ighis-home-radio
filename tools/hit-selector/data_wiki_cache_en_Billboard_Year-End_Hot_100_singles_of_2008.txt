''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' publishes annual lists of songs based on chart performance over the course of a year based on [[Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems]] and [[Nielsen SoundScan|SoundScan]] information. This is a list of the magazine's Top '''[[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]]''' songs of [[2008 in music|2008]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2008/hot-100-songs|title=Hot 100 Songs - Year-End 2008|year=2008|magazine=Billboard|access-date=March 26, 2021}}</ref>

The #1 song on the list was "[[Low (Flo Rida song)|Low]]" by [[Flo Rida]] and [[T-Pain]], after having released the song in 2007 and spent 10 weeks at number-one. The song that came in at number two was "[[Bleeding Love]]" by [[Leona Lewis]] and at #3 was [[Alicia Keys]]' song "[[No One (Alicia Keys song)|No One]]", after spending 5 weeks at #1 in December 2007.

T-Pain achieved a rare distinction of appearing on both the number-one and number one-hundred positions on a Billboard Hot 100 Year-End list.


[[File:Flo Rida at Sugar shoot.jpg|thumb|right|190px| [[Flo Rida]]'s song  "[[Low (Flo Rida song)|Low]]" with [[T-Pain]] topped the chart. He also had another song on the chart, [[In the Ayer]], a collaboration with [[will.i.am]] at 59. ]]
[[File:T-pain at hot 97 summer jam 2007.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[T-Pain]] lent his vocals to eight songs on the chart, including the best-performing song of the year, "[[Low (Flo Rida song)|Low]]," a collaboration with [[Flo Rida]].]]

[[File:Wetten dass 20130323 6475.jpg|thumb|190px|[[OneRepublic]] is the highest-charting band on the list with their single "[[Apologize (OneRepublic song)|Apologize]]" (number 5). They also had their single, "[[Stop and Stare]]" at number 33.]]

[[File:Lil Wayne in Concert.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Rapper [[Lil Wayne]] was in eight songs on the chart.  "[[Lollipop (Lil Wayne song)|Lollipop]]" was his most successful song at number 4.]]
[[File:Chris Brown & Jeg.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] singer [[Chris Brown]] had five songs on the chart, four of which are in the top 20. His best-performing song is "[[No Air]]", a duet with [[Jordin Sparks]], at number six.]]
[[File:Rihanna-brisbane-cropped2.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] had five songs on the chart. Her best-performing song was "[[Take a Bow (Rihanna song)|Take a Bow]]" at number 12.]]
[[File:Neyo.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] singer [[Ne-Yo]] had four songs on the chart, two of which are his own: "[[Closer (Ne-Yo song)|Closer]]" at number 20 and "[[Miss Independent (Ne-Yo song)|Miss Independent]]" at 57.]]
[[File:TaylorSwift 080208 photoby Adam-Bielawski.jpg|thumb|right|190px|[[Taylor Swift]] was the most successful country artist, with three songs on the chart: "[[Our Song (Taylor Swift song)|Our Song]]" at 41, "[[Teardrops on My Guitar]]" at 48, and "[[Love Story (Taylor Swift song)|Love Story]]" at 81.]]
