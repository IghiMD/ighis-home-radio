[[File:Lil Nas X back stage at the MTV Video Music Awards 2019.jpg|thumb|190px|"[[Old Town Road]]" by [[Lil Nas X]] featuring [[Billy Ray Cyrus]], the best-performing single of 2019, broke the record for the most weeks spent at number one by any song in Billboard Hot 100 history, at eighteen weeks (a nineteenth was clocked before the Billy Ray Cyrus remix was released, and thus was credited only to Lil Nas X).<ref name="yearend">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2019/hot-100-songs|title=Hot 100 Songs - Year-End 2019|year=2019|magazine=Billboard|access-date=December 6, 2019}}</ref>]]
The [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] is a chart that ranks the best-performing [[single (music)|singles]] of the United States. Its data, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine and compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]], is based collectively on each single's weekly [[CD single|physical]] and [[Digital distribution|digital]] sales, as well as [[Heavy rotation|airplay]] and [[streaming media|streaming]]. At the end of a year, ''Billboard'' will publish an annual list of the 100 most successful songs throughout that year on the Hot 100 chart based on the information.<ref name="yearend" /> For 2019, the list was published on December 5, calculated with data from November 24, 2018, to November 16, 2019. 
<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/8545496/billie-eilish-top-new-artist-2019-charts|title = The Year in Charts 2019: Billie Eilish is Top New Artist| magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] }}</ref>

[[Post Malone]] ranked as ''Billboard''{{'}}s top Hot 100 artist of 2019.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2019/hot-100-artists/|title=Hot 100 Artists - Year-End 2019|year=2019|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 25, 2022}}</ref> Of his five songs on the list, "[[Sunflower (Post Malone and Swae Lee song)|Sunflower]]" (with [[Swae Lee]]) ranked the highest, as the number-two song of the year.

==Year-end list==
[[File:Post Malone Stavernfestivalen 2018 crop.jpg|thumb|190px|Five songs from [[Post Malone]] appear on the list, with "[[Sunflower (Post Malone and Swae Lee song)|Sunflower]]" (with [[Swae Lee]]) at number 2, "[[Wow (Post Malone song)|Wow]]" at number 5, "[[Goodbyes (Post Malone song)|Goodbyes]]" (featuring [[Young Thug]]) at number 30, "[[Better Now]]" at number 32, and "[[Circles (Post Malone song)|Circles]]" at number 62.]]
[[File:Cardi B Photo by Chris Allmeid (cropped).jpg|thumb|190px|[[Cardi B]] has the most appearances of any artist on the list with six songs, three of which appear in the top 40 with "[[Girls Like You]]" ([[Maroon 5]] featuring Cardi B), "[[Please Me]]" (with [[Bruno Mars]]), and "[[Money (Cardi B song)|Money]]" at number 22, number 37, and number 38 respectively.]]
[[File:Ariana Grande (32426962484) (cropped, retouched).jpg|thumb|190px|[[Ariana Grande]] has five songs on the list, with three songs from her fifth studio album ''[[Thank U, Next]]'' placing in the top 40. "[[7 Rings]]" ranks at number 7, "[[Thank U, Next (song)|Thank U, Next]]" ranks at number 12, and "[[Break Up with Your Girlfriend, I'm Bored]]" ranks at number 36.]]
[[File:Khalid-MTV smiling.jpg|thumb|190px|[[Khalid (American singer)|Khalid]] appears on five songs on the list, three of which are in the top 20 with "[[Talk (Khalid song)|Talk]]" at number 8, "[[Eastside (song)|Eastside]] (with [[Benny Blanco]] and [[Halsey (singer)|Halsey]]) at number 17, and "[[Better (Khalid song)|Better]]" at number 20.]]
